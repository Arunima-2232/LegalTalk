Pike v Tighe 
[2018] HCA 9 
14 March 2018 
B33/2017 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside orders 2 to 5 of the orders made by the Court of Appeal of 
the Supreme Court of Queensland on 23 December 2016 and, in 
their place, order that: 
 
 (a) the appeal be dismissed; and 
 
 (b) the matter be remitted to the primary judge for the making of 
final orders. 
 
3. The first respondents pay the appellants' costs of this proceeding and 
the proceedings in the courts below.
