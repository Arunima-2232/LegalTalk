ORDER 
 
The question stated for the opinion of the Full Court in the special case filed 
on 13 August 2025 be answered as follows: 
 
Question: Do the Commonwealth and its officers have a defence to 
liability for the tort of false imprisonment with respect to the 
immigration detention of the plaintiff in the period between 
28 July 2022 and 8 November 2023? 
 
Answer:  No.
