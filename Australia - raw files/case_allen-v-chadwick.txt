Allen v Chadwick 
[2015] HCA 47 
9 December 2015

ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed in part. 
 
2. Set aside orders 1-4 of the Full Court of the Supreme Court of South 
Australia made on 10 December 2014 and, in their place, order that: 
 
(a) the appeal be allowed in part;  
 
(b) the cross-appeal be dismissed; and 
 
(c) paragraph 1 of the order made by Judge Tilmouth on 
19 November 2012 be varied by replacing the sum of 
$1,387,888.87 with the sum of  $1,223,287.74. 
 
3. Appellant to pay the respondent's costs of and incidental to the 
appeal to this Court.
