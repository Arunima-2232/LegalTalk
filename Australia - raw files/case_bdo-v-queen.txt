B52/2022 
 
ORDER 
 
1.  Appeal allowed with respect to counts 2, 3, 4, 7 and 8.  
 
2.  With respect to those counts, set aside order 1 of the Court of Appeal 
of the Supreme Court of Queensland made on 15 October 2021, and 
in its place enter a judgment and verdict of acquittal.  
 
3. Set aside order 3 of the Court of Appeal. 
 
4.  Remit the matter to the Court of Appeal for resentencing of the 
appellant.
