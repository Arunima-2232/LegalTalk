CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Peters v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Conspiracy to defraud – Elements of – Whether dishonesty an 
essential element – Tests of dishonesty in R v Ghosh and R v Salvo – Actus reus 
and mens rea – Agreement to use dishonest means – Intention to prejudice or 
imperil the rights or interest of others – Direction to jury. 
 
Crimes Act 1914 (Cth), ss 86(1)(e) and 86A.
