CATCHWORDS 
 
New South Wales v Bujdoso  
 
Negligence – Breach of duty of care – Prison authorities – Respondent was a 
prisoner admitted to minimal supervision Work Release Programme – 
Respondent had been threatened by other prisoners – Appellant knew of threats – 
Respondent assaulted by a group of prisoners – Scope of duty of prison 
authorities to protect the safety of prisoners under its control – Whether the 
appellant breached its duty of care to the respondent – Whether effective 
measures were adopted to prevent a foreseeable risk of injury to the respondent.
