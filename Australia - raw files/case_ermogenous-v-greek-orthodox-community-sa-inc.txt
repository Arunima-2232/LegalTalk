ORDER 
 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside paragraphs 3 to 6 inclusive of the order of the Full Court of the 
Supreme Court of South Australia dated 5 October 2000. 
 
3. Remit the matter to that Court for further hearing and determination 
conformably with the reasons of this Court.
