Cassegrain v Gerard Cassegrain & Co Pty Ltd 
[2015] HCA 2 
4 February 2015 
S141/2014 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed in part.  
 
2. Set aside paragraphs 2 and 3 of the order of the Court of Appeal of 
the Supreme Court of New South Wales made on 18 December 2013 
and, in their place:  
 
(a) declare that the appellant holds a half interest in the property 
described in F olio Identifiers 4/792413, 1/798316, 
115/754434, 124/754434, 2/720827, 117/754434, 118/754434  
and 174/754434 on trust for the respondent absolutely; and 
 
(b) order that the appellant execute a Real Property Act  1900 
(NSW) transfer of a one -half interest in  the property referred 
to in paragraph (a) to the respondent.   
 
3. The respondent pay the appellant's costs of the appeal to this Court.
