CATCHWORDS 
 
Palmer Bruyn & Parker Pty Ltd v Parsons 
 
Injurious falsehood –  Elements of the tort – Forged letter containing false 
statements – Initial publication to defined group intended to ridicule subject of 
letter – Report in newspaper of "bogus letter" – Contract terminated as a result of 
newspaper report – Whether loss suffered caused by initial publication – Whether 
loss suffered was a natural and probable consequence of initial publication – 
Identification of relevant falsehood – Relevance of reasonable foreseeability as 
criterion for limiting liability – Causation of plaintiff's damage – Whether actual 
damage to plaintiff proved or assumed by expert report. 
 
Words and phrases – "Natural and probable consequence", "grapevine effect".
