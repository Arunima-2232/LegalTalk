ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside para graphs 2 and 3 of the orders of the Full Court of the 
Supreme Court of Western Australia made on 13 October 2004 and, in 
their place, order that: 
 
(a)  the judgment and orders of the District Court of Western Australia 
made on 17 December 2002 be set aside; and 
 
(b)  there be a new trial of the action.

CATCHWORDS 
 
CSR Ltd v Della Maddalena 
 
Practice and procedure – Appeal – Credibility of witness – Whether intermediate 
appellate court entitled to substitute its own findings as to credibility for that of 
trial judge – Whether court erred in ordering retrial limited to assessment of 
damages.  
 
Courts – Appeal – Procedural fairness –  Expert witnesses – Court expressed 
preference for evidence of particular expert witnesses – Whether court's 
reference to such expert witnesses as "well known to the court" constituted a 
breach of procedural fairness – Whether matter should be remitted for rehearing. 
 
Words and phrases – "procedural fairness", "retrial". 
 
Supreme Court Act 1935 (WA), s 58(1)(a).
