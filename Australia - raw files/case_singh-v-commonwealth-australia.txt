ORDER 
 
Questions in the case stated answered as follows: 
 
1. Q. Is the plaintiff an alien within the meaning of s 51(xix) of the 
Constitution? 
A. Yes. 
 
2. Q. If the answer to (1) is "No", is s 198 of the Migration Act 1958 
(Cth) capable of valid application to the plaintiff? 
A. Does not arise. 
 
3. Q. By whom should the costs of the case stated to the Full Court of 
this Court be borne? 
A. The plaintiff.
