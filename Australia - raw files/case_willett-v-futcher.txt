ORDER 
 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside order of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Queensland made on 20 February 2004 and in its place order that the 
appeal to that Court is allowed with costs. 
 
3. Remit matter to the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of Queensland 
for the assessment of the damages to be allowed. 
 
4. Amend title to the proceeding in this Court by deleting the words "an 
infant".
