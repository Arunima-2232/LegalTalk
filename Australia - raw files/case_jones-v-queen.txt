CATCHWORDS 
 
Jones v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Evidence – Joint murder trial – Admissibility of evidence 
adduced by accused of bad character or propensity of co-accused – Whether 
appellant prevented by trial judge from fully adducing relevant admissible 
evidence – Whether jury misdirected as to use of evidence of appellant's bad 
character. 
 
Criminal law – Appeals – Application of proviso. 
 
Words and phrases – "substantial miscarriage of justice". 
 
Criminal Code (Q), s 668E(1A).
