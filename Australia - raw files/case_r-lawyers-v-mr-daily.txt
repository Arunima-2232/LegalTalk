ORDER 
 
1. Grant leave to the appellant to file an amended notice of appeal. 
 
2. Appeal allowed. 
 
3. Set aside orders 2, 3 and 4 of the orders made by the Full Court of the 
Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (Division 1) on 
17 October 2024 and, in their place, order that: 
 
(a) the appeal be dismissed; and  
 
(b) the appellant pay the second respondent's costs of the appeal. 
 
4. The first respondent pay the appellant's costs of the appeal.
