CATCHWORDS 
 
Ryan v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Sentencing – Sexual offences against children – Where appellant 
disclosed a large number of offences to police – Whether disclosure entitled 
appellant to a significantly discounted sentence – Whether the likelihood of 
disclosed offences being otherwise discovered should have been assessed. 
 
Criminal law – Sentencing – Use of character in the sentencing process – 
Whether the appellant's otherwise good character entitled him to some leniency.
