CATCHWORDS 
 
Blank v Commissioner of Taxation 
 
Income tax – Assessable income – Where taxpayer participated in employee 
incentive profit participation agreement – Taxpayer granted claim to deferred 
compensation calculated on basis of company pr ofit – Amount payable under 
agreement to taxpayer on termination of employment and execution of 
declaration of assignment and release – Whether amount income according to 
ordinary concepts or capital gain. 
 
Words and phrases – "deferred compensation", " Genussscheine", "incentive 
profit participation agreement", "ordinary income", "pecuniary account".

Swiss Code of Obligations, Art 657.
