Queanbeyan City Council v ACTEW Corporation Ltd [2011] HCA 40 
5 October 2011 
C2/2011 & C3/2011 
 
ORDER 
 
Matter No C2/2011 
 
1. Set aside sub-paragraphs 8(a) and 8(b) of the Order of the Full Court of the 
Federal Court of Australia made on 29 October 2010 and, in their place, 
order that each party bear its own costs of the appeal and the cross- appeal 
to that Court. 
 
2. Otherwise, appeal dismissed.  
 
Matter No C3/2011 
 
1. Set aside sub-paragraphs 8(a) and 8(b) of the Order of the Full Court of the 
Federal Court of Australia made on 29 October 2010 and, in their place, 
order that each party bear its own costs of the appeal to that Court. 
 
2. Otherwise, appeal dismissed.
