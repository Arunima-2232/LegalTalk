Walsh v Law Society of New South Wales [1999] HCA 33 
5 August 1999 
S136/1998 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders made by the New South Wales Court of Appeal on 
15 December 1997, except for those orders numbered 6 and 7, and in 
place of the orders set aside order that the New South Wales Court of 
Appeal hear and determine the appeal and cross-appeal to that court 
conformably with the reasons for judgment of this Court. 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of New South Wales

D F Jackson QC with J J Priestley for the appellant (instructed by Stockman 
& Evans) 
 
J Basten QC for the respondent (instructed by Law Society of New South 
Wales)
