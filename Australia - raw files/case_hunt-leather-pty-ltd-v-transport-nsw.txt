ORDER 
 
In Matter No S20/2025 
 
1. Appeal allowed in part. 
 
2. The orders of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New South 
Wales made on 18 September 2024 be set aside and, in their place, it 
be ordered that the appeal and cross-appeal be dismissed with costs.  
 
3. The respondent pay the appellants' costs of the appeal.
