Attwells v Jackson Lalic Lawyers Pty Limited 
[2016] HCA 16 
4 May 2016 
S161/2015 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside orders 3, 4 and 5 of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of New South Wales made on 1 October 2014 and in their 
place order that: 
 
(a) the appeal be allowed; 
 
(b) the orders of Harrison J made on 17 Octob er 2013 be set 
aside, and in their place order that the separate question of 
whether the plaintiffs' claim is defeated entirely, because the 
defendant is immune from suit, be answered:  "No".  
 
3. Set aside the orders of the Supreme Court of New South Wale s made 
on 28 October 2014. 
 
4. The respondent pay the appellants' costs of the proceedings on the 
separate question in the courts below and of the appeal to this Court. 
 
5. The Law Society of New South Wales pay the costs of the appellants 
and the respondent occasioned by its intervention in the proceedings.
