Toll (FGCT) Pty Limited v Alphapharm Pty Limited [2004] HCA 52 
11 November 2004 
S63/2004 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside order 1 of the orders of the New South Wales Court of Appeal 
and, in lieu thereof, order that the appeal to that Court be allowed with 
costs. 
 
3. Set aside orders 1, 2 and 4 of the trial judge and in lieu thereof, order 
that: 
 
 (a) the First and Second Plaintiffs' claim in the District Court of New 
South Wales be dismissed; 
 
 (b) judgment be entered for the Defendant; 
 
 (c) the First and Second Plaintiffs pay the Defendant's costs of the 
trial; and 
 
 (d) the First and Second Plaintiffs pay the costs of the First and Third 
Cross Defendants. 
 
4. Order that the First Respondent repay to the Appellant the sum of 
$683,061.86 together with interest from the date of payment at rates  under 
the District Court Act 1973 (NSW) or as agreed.
