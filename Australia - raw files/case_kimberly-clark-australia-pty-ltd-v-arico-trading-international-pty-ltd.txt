ORDER 
 
 
1.  Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2.  Set aside Orders 1, 2 and 3 of the orders of the Full Court of the Federal 
Court of Australia on 30 August 1999 and in lieu thereof order that the 
matter be remitted to the Full Court of the Federal Court of Australia for 
determination of the remaining grounds of appeal in that Court, excluding 
any grounds previously abandoned in that Court. 
 
3.  Costs in the Federal Court of Australia to abide the outcome of the further 
hearing in that Court.
