CATCHWORDS 
 
Wigmans v AMP Limited 
 
Practice and procedure – Representative action – Stay – Where five open class 
representative actions commenced against same defendant in relation to same 
controversy – Where considerable overlap between claims made in proceedings – 
Where representative plaintiff in four proceedings filed notice of motion in 
Supreme Court of New South Wales seeking orders that each other proceeding be 
permanently stayed – Whether Supreme Court's power to grant stay is confined by 
rule or presumption th at representative proceeding issued first in time is to be 
preferred – Whether litigation funding arrangements can be relevant consideration 
under s 67 of Civil Procedure Act 2005 (NSW) – Whether Supreme Court erred in 
considering litigation funding arrangements. 
 
Words and phrases – "abuse of process", "auction process", "certification and 
carriage motion procedures", "class actions", "competing funding proposals, costs 
estimates and net hypothetical return to members", "competing representative 
proceedings", "conflicts of interest", "contradictor", "duplicative proceedings", 
"equitable principles concerning test actions", "first-in-time rule or presumption", 
"funding model", "litigation funding arrangements", "multifactorial approach", 
"multiplicity", "one size fits all", "power to grant a stay", "prima facie vexatious 
and oppressive", "representative proceedings", "special referee". 
 
Civil Procedure Act 2005 (NSW), ss 56, 57, 58, 67, Pt 10.
