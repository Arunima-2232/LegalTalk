Edwards v Santos Limited [2011] HCA 8 
30 March 2011 
S153/2010 
 
ORDER 
 
1. A writ of certiorari issue directed to the fourth defendant to quash: 
 
 (a) the decisions of the Federal Court of Australia made on 18 December 
2009 and 17 March 2010 in proceeding QUD 86 of 2009; and  
 
 (b) the decision of the Full Court of the Federal Court made on 4 June 
2010 in proceeding QUD 28 of 2010. 
 
2. Dismiss the application for the issue of a writ of mandamus against the 
fourth defendant.  
 
3. The first, second and third defendants pay the costs of the plaintiffs in: 
 
 (a) the Federal Court of and incident al to the first, second and third 
defendants' motions for summary dismissal; 
 
 (b) the Full Court of the Federal Court; and  
 
 (c) this Court.
