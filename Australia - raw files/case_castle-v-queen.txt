ORDER 
 
Matter No A24/2016 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Criminal Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of South Australia made on 3 December 2015, and in its place 
order that:  
 
(a) the appeal be allowed; 
 
(b) the appellant's conviction be quashed; and  
 
(c) a new trial be had.

Court of South Australia made on 3 December 20 15, and in its place 
order that:  
 
(a) the appeal be allowed; 
 
(b) the appellant's conviction be quashed; and  
 
(c) a new trial be had.
