CATCHWORDS 
 
Pfeiffer v Stevens 
 
Statutes – Construction – By-laws and Regulations – Validity – Procedure for 
making interim local laws – Whether Minister's power to extend interim local 
law limited so as to preclude multiple extensions – Whether power to extend 
interim local law contrary to requirements of sunset provision – Where statute 
conferred power on Minister to extend interim local law for "longer period" –  
Whether interim local law had ceased to have effect at relevant time. 
 
Local Government – By-laws and Regulations – Validity – Power of extension of 
interim local law. 
 
Constitutional law (Cth) – "law of a State" – Constitutional presumptions and 
penal laws – Contextual implications of limited delegation – Whether multiple 
extensions of an interim local law that imposes penal sanctions results in 
uncertain validity. 
 
Words and phrases – "sunset provision", "a longer period", "interim local law". 
 
Acts Interpretation Act 1954 (Q), s 23(1). 
Local Government Act 1993 (Q), ss 850, 851, 859, 860, 861, 862, 863.
