ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New 
South Wales dated 11 October 1999 and in lieu thereof order that: 
 
a) the appeal to that Court be allowed; 
 
b) the orders and declaration of the District Court of New South Wales be 
set aside; and  
 
c) judgment be entered for the defendant. 
 
3. Appellant to pay the respondent's costs in the District Court, the Court of 
Appeal and this Court.

CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Derrick v Cheung  
 
 
Negligence – Standard of care of motorist – Vehicle struck infant who darted on 
to road – Vehicle travelling at 10 to 15 kilometres per hour under the speed limit 
– Whether Court of Appeal was entitled to find that the motorist was travelling at 
excessive speed in the circumstances.
