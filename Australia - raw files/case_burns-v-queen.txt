ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed.  
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Criminal Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of New South Wales made on 1 April 2011, and in lieu thereof 
order that:  
 
(a) the appeal to that Court be allowed;  
 
(b) the appellant's conviction for the manslaughter of David Hay 
be quashed; and 
 
(c) a verdict of acquittal be entered.
