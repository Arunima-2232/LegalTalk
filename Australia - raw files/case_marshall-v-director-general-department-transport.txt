ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs including reserved costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders made by the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Queensland on 22 October 1999, and in place thereof, order that: 
 
 (a) the appeal to that Court be allowed with costs; 
 
(b) the order of the Land Appeal Court made on 24 July 1998 be set 
aside; 
 
(c) the application dismissed by the Land Appeal Court be remitted to 
that Court to be dealt with in accordance with law.

CATCHWORDS 
 
Marshall v Director-General, Department of Transport 
 
Real property – Resumption of land by public body – Compensation – Whether 
compensation for injurious affection is payable in respect of land taken for the 
statutory purpose but not, or not yet, physically used for that purpose. 
 
Words and phrases – "injurious affection" – "compensation" – "depreciation" – 
"severed land" – "residual land" – "road purposes". 
 
Acquisition of Land Act 1967 (Q), ss 4, 7, 12, 20. 
Land Act 1962 (Q) ss 44, 45. 
Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845 (UK), s 63. 
Lands Acquisition Act 1955 (Cth), s 23.
