CATCHWORDS 
 
The Queen v Dookheea 
 
Criminal law – Criminal procedure – Jury directions – Standard of proof – Where 
jury directed that Crown required to prove accused's guilt not beyond any doubt 
but beyond reasonable doubt – Whether such direction error of law – Whether 
such direction productive of substantial miscarriage of justice.       
 
Words and phrases – "any doubt", "beyond reasonable doubt", "fanciful doubt".
