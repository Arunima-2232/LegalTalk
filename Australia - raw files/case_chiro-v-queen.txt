Chiro v The Queen 
[2017] HCA 37 
13 September 2017 
A9/2017 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed in part. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of  Criminal Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of South Australia made on 30 September 2015 in SCCRM -15-
232, and in its place order that the appeal against sentence be 
allowed and the sentence be set aside. 
 
3. Remit the proceeding to the Court of Criminal Appeal o f the 
Supreme Court of South Australia for the appellant to be resentenced 
in accordance with the reasons of this Court. 
 
4. Appeal otherwise dismissed.
