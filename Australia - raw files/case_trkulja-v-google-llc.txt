Trkulja v Google LLC 
[2018] HCA 25 
13 June 2018 
M88/2017 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Pursuant to r 42.07.1 of the High Cou rt Rules 2004 (Cth) 
Google LLC is made the respondent to this appeal in substitution for 
Google Inc. 
 
2. Appeal allowed. 
 
3. Set aside orders 2 to 6  of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court 
of Victoria made on 20 December 2016  and, in their place , order 
that the appeal be dismissed with costs.  
 
4.  The respondent pay the appellant's costs of the appeal to this Court.
