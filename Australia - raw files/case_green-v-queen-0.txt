CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Green v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Conspiracy to murder – Alleged lies told by accused – Lies as 
corroboration – Direction to jury – Failure to direct that lies could not be used as 
corroboration of witness where evidence of same witness is used to establish the 
lie – Other evidence upon which jury could conclude accused was telling lies – No 
substantial miscarriage of justice – Application of proviso. 
 
Edwards v The Queen (1993) 178 CLR 193.
