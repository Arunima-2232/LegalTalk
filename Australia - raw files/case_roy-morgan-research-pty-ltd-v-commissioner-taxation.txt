CATCHWORDS 
 
Roy Morgan Research Pty Ltd v Commissioner of Taxation 
 
Constitutional law (Cth) – Taxation – s 51(ii) – Superannuation guarantee charge 
imposed upon employers who fail to provide to employees a prescribed level of 
superannuation – Charge debt due to Commonwealth and paid into Consolidated 
Revenue Fund for benefit of employees – Whether law imposing charge not a 
law with respect to taxation because charge is not imposed for "public purposes", 
and because it confers a "private and direct benefit" on employees of those 
employers who pay charge.  
 
Words and phrases – "charge", "compulsory exaction", "private and direct 
benefit", "public purposes".  
 
Constitution, s 51(ii).  
Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 (Cth), ss 16, 17. 
Superannuation Guarantee Charge Act 1992 (Cth), ss 5, 6.
