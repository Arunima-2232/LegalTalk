CATCHWORDS 
 
Golden Eagle International Trading Pty Ltd v Zhang  
 
Damages – s 45 of the Motor Accidents Act 1988 (NSW) obliged an insurer to 
make certain payments to or on behalf of the victim of a motor accident once the 
insurer admitted liability to the victim – Treatment of these payments in 
assessing da mages – Whether these payments should be removed from the 
calculation before or after reducing on account of contributory negligence the 
amount of damages assessed – What onus of proof should apply to a dispute over 
such damages. 
 
Damages – Assessment of life expectancy – Whether reference should be made 
to "projected" or "historical" life expectancy tables. 
 
Words and phrases – "best evidence rule", "contributory negligence", "damages 
payable", "damages recoverable", "defence". 
 
Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1965 (NSW). 
Motor Accidents Act 1988 (NSW), ss 2A, 45, 74. 
Motor Accidents Amendment Act 1995 (NSW). 
Workers' Compensation Act 1926 (NSW).
