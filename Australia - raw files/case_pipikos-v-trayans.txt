CATCHWORDS 
 
Pipikos v Trayans 
 
Equity – Doctrine of part performance – Where respondent sole reg istered 
proprietor of property purchased by respondent and her husband – Where 
respondent and her husband made improvements to property – Where appellant 
claimed agreement between appellant and respondent entitled appellant to 
half-interest in unimproved l and – Where alleged agreement did not meet 
formality requirements of s  26(1) of Law of Property Act 1936 (SA) – Where 
s 26(2) of Law of Property Act provides that s 26 does not affect law relating to 
part performance – Whether acts of part performance enti tled appellant to 
specific performance of alleged agreement – Whether acts of part performance 
must be unequivocally, and in their own nature, referable to agreement of kind 
alleged – Whether sufficient for purposes of doctrine of part performance to 
establish that contracting party has knowingly been induced or allowed by 
counterparty to alter his or her position on faith of contract.
