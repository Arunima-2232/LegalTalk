CATCHWORDS 
 
 
James Hardie & Coy Pty Limited v Seltsam Pty Limited 
 
Tort – Contribution – Parties entitled to claim contribution – Parties against whom 
contribution can be claimed – Relevance of final judgment obtained by consent in 
favour of party against whom contribution claimed. 
 
Tort – Contribution – Assessment – Relevance of liability of claimant tortfeasor to 
tort victim when determining liability between claimant tortfeasor and other 
tortfeasors. 
 
Statutes – Construction – Purposive construction – History of legislation –  
Previous decisions on the statutory provision. 
 
Words and phrases – "tort-feasor liable in respect of that damage" –  "who is, or 
would if sued have been, liable in respect of the same damage".
