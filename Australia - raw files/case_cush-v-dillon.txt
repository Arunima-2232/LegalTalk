CATCHWORDS 
 
Cush v Dillon 
Boland v Dillon 
 
Defamation – Defence of qualified privilege – Where occasion of qualified 
privilege existed to communicate existence of rumour – Where defendant 
published rumour as "common knowledge" – Whether matter published on 
occasion attracting defence of qualified privilege – Whether distinction between 
publication of rumour and publication of fact of rumour. 
 
Defamation – Defence of qualified privilege – Rebuttal by express malice –  
Where defendant did not believe truth of publication – Whether lack of belief in 
truth of publication sufficient to establish malice.  
 
Words and phrases – "express malice", "qualified privilege". 
 
Defamation Act 1974 (NSW), s 7A.
