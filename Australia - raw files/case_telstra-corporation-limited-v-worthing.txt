Telstra Corporation Limited v Worthing (S68-1998) [1999] HCA 12 
24 March 1999 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Order 3 of the orders of the Court of Appeal entered on 31 March 1998 
be set aside and in lieu thereof order that the appeal to that Court be 
allowed, Order 1 made by Truss CCJ on 10 July 1997 be set aside, and 
the application for determination dated 10 September 1996 be struck 
out for want of jurisdiction. 
 
3. The appellant pay the first respondent's costs of the appeal to this Court.
