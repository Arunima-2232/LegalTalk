Date of Order:  2 December 1997 
Reasons for Judgment Delivered:  20 January 1998 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal and in lieu thereof grant leave to 
appeal to that Court, allow the appeal and quash the appellant's convictions 
and enter verdicts of acquittal in their place.
