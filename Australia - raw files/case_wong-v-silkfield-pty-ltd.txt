B26/1999 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside order 2 of the orders of the Full Court of the Federal Court of 
Australia made 20 November 1998, together with the declaration made 
by that Court, and in lieu thereof, save as to the direction by that Court 
as to the future conduct and procedure in the matter, order that the 
appeal to that Court be dismissed with costs.
