O'Dea v Western Australia 
[2022] HCA 24 
Date of Hearing: 4 May 2022 
Date of Judgment: 10 August 2022 
P53/2021 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Western Australia made on 13 April 2021 and, in their place, order 
that: 
 
(a) there be an extension of time within which to apply for leave to 
appeal; 
 
(b) leave to appeal be granted; 
 
(c) the appeal be allowed; 
 
(d) the appellant's conviction be set aside; and 
 
(e) there be a new trial. 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of Western Australia
