CATCHWORDS 
 
Commonwealth Director of Public Prosecutions v Poniatowska 
 
Criminal law –  Physical element of offence – Omission – Respondent convicted 
of multiple charges of obtaining financial advantage from Commonwealth entity 
contrary to s 135.2(1) of Criminal Code (Cth) ("Code") –  Respondent failed to 
advise Centrelink of receipt of payments of commission from employer –  
Whether omission to perform act that person not under legal obligation to 
perform can be physical element of offence created b y s 135.2(1) of Code – 
Whether s 4.3 of Code gave expression to common law principle that criminal 
liability does not attach to omission unless it is omission to perform act that 
person is under legal obligation to perform.   
 
Words and phrases – "engages in conduct", "makes it so", "obtains a financial 
advantage", "omission".  
 
Criminal Code (Cth), ss 4.1(2), 4.3, 135.2(1). 
Social Security Act 1991 (Cth), Pt 2.10, s 1068A. 
Social Security (Administration) Act 1999 (Cth), s 74.
