ORDER 
 
In each matter: 
 
1. The action be permanently stayed. 
 
2. The respondent plaintiff or plaintiffs pay the applicant defendant's costs of 
the action (including this application) on an indemnity basis. 
 
3. In action B5 of 2000 I order that: 
 
 (i) each party file any further material intended to be relied upon 
before 25 September next; 
 
 (ii) that written submissions be filed by each party before 10 October 
next; and 
 
 (iii) that the applicant's summons be adjourned to a date to be fixed.
