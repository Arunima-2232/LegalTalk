Pearce v The Queen (S87-1997) [1998] HCA 57  
10 September 1998 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2.  Set aside the order of the Court of Criminal Appeal dismissing the 
appellant's application for leave to appeal against sentence. 
 
3.  Remit the matter to the Court of Criminal Appeal to be dealt with  
consistently with the reasons for judgment of this Court.

CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Pearce v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Double jeopardy – Appellant charged with and convicted of two 
offences arising out of same facts – Whether plea in bar available – Whether an 
abuse of process – Whether double punishment. 
 
Crimes Act 1900 (NSW), ss 33, 110.
