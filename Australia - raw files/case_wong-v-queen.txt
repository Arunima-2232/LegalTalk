Wong v The Queen 
[2001] HCA 64 
15 November 2001 
S193/2000 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order  of the Court of Criminal Appeal of New South Wales 
dated 16 December 1999. 
 
3. Remit the matter to that Court for further hearing and determination 
conformably with the reasons of this Court.
