CATCHWORDS 
 
KRM v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Practice and procedure – Sexual offences – Multiple counts – 
One count of maintaining relationship with child under 16 pursuant to s 47A 
Crimes Act 1958 (Vic) – Whether propensity warning required when presentment 
involves multiple counts of sexual offences and relationship count pursuant to 
s 47A – Whether propensity warning required in respect of individual acts that 
form basis of a charge under s 47A – Use of "separate consideration" warning –  
Operation of proviso. 
 
Criminal law – Sexual offences – s 47A Crimes Act 1958 (Vic) – Whether 
legislature can modify the need for particulars of criminal charges – Whether 
evidence of uncharged sexual acts admissible to prove the nature of the 
relationship between the accused and the complainant. 
 
Evidence – Propensity evidence – "No rational view" test – Application to 
relationship evidence. 
 
Crimes Act 1958 (Vic), s 47A.
