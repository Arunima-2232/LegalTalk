CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Hoyts Pty Ltd v Burns 
 
 
Negligence – Causation – Failure to warn customers that cinema seats retracted 
automatically – Respondent injured attempting to sit on a retracted seat – 
Whether Court of Appeal erred in interfering in primary judge's finding that 
warning sign would not have altered respondent's conduct. 
 
Appeal – Rehearing before Court of Appeal –  Relevance of credibility findings –  
Whether primary judge's conclusion based upon assessment of credibility of 
party – Whether Court of Appeal erred in disturbing such conclusion – Whether 
Court of Appeal omitted to find error before substituting its own conclusion on 
the evidence.
