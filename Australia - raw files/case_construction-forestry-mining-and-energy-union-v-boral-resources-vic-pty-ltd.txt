brought under r  75.06(2) of Supreme Court (General Civil Procedure) Rules 
2005 (Vic) ("Rules") to punish appellant for contempt of court – Where appellant 
is a corporation – Whether a corporation may be ordered to make discovery 
under r  29.07(2) of Rules in a contempt proceeding – Whether a contempt 
proceeding is a criminal proceeding or a civil proceeding.

principle", "criminal proceeding".
