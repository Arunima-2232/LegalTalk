Fleming v The Queen (S59-1998) [1998] HCA 68 
Date of Order:  9 October 1998 
Date of Publication of Reasons:  11 November 1998 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Criminal Appeal of New South Wales.  
In lieu thereof order that the appeal to that Court be allowed, the 
convictions be quashed and there be a new trial on all counts on which 
the appellant was convicted. 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of New South Wales

CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Mark Fleming v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Appeal – Trial by judge sitting alone – Failure by judge to include 
in reasons for judgment a warning required to be given to jury – Whether failure 
demonstrates a breach of Criminal Procedure Act 1986 (NSW), s 33(3) – Whether 
failure amounted to a wrong decision on a question of law – Whether, despite 
failure, no substantial miscarriage of justice occurred. 
 
Criminal law – Appeal under Criminal Appeal Act 1912 (NSW), s 6 – Nature of 
appeal – Relevance of phrase "unsafe and unsatisfactory". 
 
Criminal Appeal Act 1912 (NSW), ss 5, 6. 
 
Criminal Procedure Act 1986 (NSW), Pt 9 (ss 30-33).
