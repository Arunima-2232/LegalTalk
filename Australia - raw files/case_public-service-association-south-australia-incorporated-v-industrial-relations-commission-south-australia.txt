Public Service Association of South Australia Incorporated v Industrial 
Relations Commission of South Australia [2012] HCA 25 
11 July 2012 
A7/2011 
 
ORDER 
 
 
1. Special leave to appeal granted.  
 
2. Appeal treated as instituted and heard instanter, and allowed. 
 
3. Set aside the orders of the Full Court of the Supreme Court of South 
Australia made on 15 March 2011. 
 
4. Remit the matter to the Full Court of the Supreme Court of South 
Australia for determination of the appellant's summons for judicial 
review, including any questions of costs in the Full Court. 
 
5. The second respondent, the Chief Executive, Department of the Premier 
and Cabinet of South Australia, pay the costs of the appellant in this 
Court.
