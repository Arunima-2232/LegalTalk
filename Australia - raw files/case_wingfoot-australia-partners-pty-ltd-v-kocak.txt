ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed.  
 
2. Set aside orders 1, 2 and 3 of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of Victoria made on 23 October 2012 and, in their place, 
order that the appeal to that Court be dismissed. 
 
3. The appellants pay the first respondent' s costs of this appeal and the 
application for special leave to appeal.
