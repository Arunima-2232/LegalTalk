Roncevich v Repatriation Commission 
[2005] HCA 40 
10 August 2005 
D7/2004 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Full Court of the Federal Court of Australia 
dated 30 June 2003 and, in place thereof, order that: 
 
 (a) the appeal be allowed; 
 
 (b) the order of Mansfield J in the Federal Court of Australia dated 2 
December 2002 be set aside and, in place thereof, order that:  
 
  (i) the appeal be allowed; 
  
  (ii) the decision of the Tribunal be set aside; and 
 
  (iii) the matter be remitted to the Tribunal to be determined 
according to law. 
 
3. The respondent is to pay the appellant's costs in the Federal Court, both 
at first instance and in the Full Court, and the appellant's costs of the 
appeal to this Court.  
 
 
On appeal from the Federal Court of Australia
