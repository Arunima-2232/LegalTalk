ORDER 
 
The questions of law stated for the opinion of the Full Court in the Special 
Case filed on 3 December 2021 be answered as follows: 
 
1. Are any of items 7, 9, 11 and  14 of Sch 1 to the Electoral Legislation 
Amendment (Party Registration Integrity) Act 2021  (Cth) invalid, in 
whole or in part, on the ground that they infringe the implied freedom 
of political communication? 
 
Answer:  
 
In relation to items 11 and 14 of Sc h 1 to the Electoral Legislation 
Amendment (Party Registration Integrity) Act 2021 (Cth), the answer 
is "No". Otherwise unnecessary to answer. 
 
2. Are any of items 7, 9, 11 and 14 of Sch 1 to the Electoral Legislation 
Amendment (Party Registration Integrity) Act 2021  (Cth) invalid, in 
whole or in part, on the ground that they preclude the direct choice by 
the people of Senators and Members of the House of Representatives, 
contrary to ss 7 and 24 of the Constitution? 
    2. 
 
Answer:  
 
In relation to items 11 and 14 of Sch 1 to the Electoral Legislation 
Amendment (Party Registration Integrity) Act 2021 (Cth), the answer 
is "No". Otherwise unnecessary to answer. 
 
3. In light of the answers to Questions 1 and 2, what relief, if any, should 
issue? 
 
Answer: 
 
None.  
 
4. Who should pay the costs of and incidental to this special case? 
 
Answer: 
 
The plaintiff.
