ORDER 
 
1. Each of the appeals is allowed. 
 
2. Paragraphs 4 and 6 of the orders of the Full Court of the Federal Court made 
on 3 March 2000, the whole of the order of the Full Court of the Federal 
Court made on 11 May 2000 and the determination of native title made on 
11 May 2000 are set aside and the matters remitted to the Full Court for 
further hearing and determination. 
 
3. There is no order as to the costs of the appeals in this Court. 
 
4. The costs of the proceedings at trial and in the Full Court of the Federal 
Court, both before and after the making of this Court's orders disposing of 
these appeals, are to be in the discretion of the Full Court.
