ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2.  Set aside the order made in paragraph 1 of the order of the Court of 
Appeal of the Supreme Court of the Northern Territory made on 
19 October 2017 and the order made in paragraph 1 of the order 
made on 28 March 201 8 and, in their place, order that the appeal to 
that Court be dismissed with costs. 
 
3.  The first respondent pay the appellant's costs of the appeal to this 
Court.
