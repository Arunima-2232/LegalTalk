Attorney-General (NT) v Emmerson 
[2014] HCA 13 
10 April 2014 
D5/2013 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of the Northern Territory 
made on 28 March 2013 and paragraphs 1, 2 and 4 (first appearing) 
of the order of the Court of Appeal made on 13 May 2013 and, in 
their place, order that the appeal to that Court be otherwise 
dismissed with costs. 
 
3. The second appellant pay the first respondent's costs in this Court.
