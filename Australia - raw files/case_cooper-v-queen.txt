Cooper v The Queen 
[2012] HCA 50 
14 November 2012

ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Criminal Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of New South Wales made on 5 December 2011 and, in its 
place, order that: 
 
(a)  the appeal to that Court be allowed; 
 
(b) the appellant's conviction be quashed; and 
 
(c) a new trial be had.
