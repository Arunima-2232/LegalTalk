CATCHWORDS 
 
Badenach v Calvert 
 
Negligence – Duty of care – Scope of duty of care – Where solicitor received 
instructions from testator to prepare a will – Where entirety of testator's estate 
was to pass to respondent – Where testator's daughter brought successful 
proceedings under Testator's Family Maintenance Act 1912 (Tas) ("TFM Act") 
for provision out of testator's estate – Whether duty of care owed by solicitor to 
testator extended to advising testator of possible steps to avoid exposing testator's 
estate to a claim under TFM Act. 
 
Negligence – Duty of care – Existence of duty of care – Whether solicitor owed 
duty of care to intended beneficiary under testator's will – Whether Hill v Van 
Erp (1997) 188 CLR 159 applied – Whether interests of testator coincident with 
interests of intended beneficiary. 
 
Negligence – Causation – Whether, but for solicitor's failure to give advice, 
respondent would have received entirety of testator's estate – Whether relevant 
loss is a loss of chance. 
 
Words and phrases – "coincident", "duty of care", "interests of the intended 
beneficiary", "interests of the testator", "loss of a chance", "testamentary 
intention". 
 
Civil Liability Act 2002 (Tas), s 13(1)(a). 
Testator's Family Maintenance Act 1912 (Tas).
