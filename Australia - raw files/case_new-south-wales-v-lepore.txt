New South Wales v Lepore [2003] HCA 4 
6 February 2003 
S108/2002 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed in part. 
 
2. Paragraph 2 of the order of the Court of Appeal of New South Wales made on 
23 April 2001 set aside, and in its place, order that the judgment entered in 
the District Court on 16 April 1999 be wholly set aside and that there be a 
new trial. 
 
3. Appellant to pay the costs of the appeal to this Court.  
 
4. Costs of the new trial to abide its outcome.
