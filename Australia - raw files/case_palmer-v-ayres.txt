ORDER 
 
In each matter: 
 
1. The question reserved for the consideration of the Full Court 
pursuant to s 18 of the Judiciary Act  1903 (Cth) is answered as 
follows: 
  
 
  
2. 
 
 Question: Is s 596A of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) invalid as 
contrary to Chapter  III of the Constitution in that it 
confers non -judicial power on federal courts and on 
courts exercising federal jurisdiction? 
 
 Answer: No. 
 
2. The writ of summons is dismissed. 
 
3. The plaintiff pay the defendants' costs.
