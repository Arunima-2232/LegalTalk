ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside order 2 of the orders of the Court of Criminal Appeal of New 
South Wales and in lieu thereof allow the appeal to that Court. 
 
3. Remit the matter to the Court of Criminal Appeal of New South Wales 
to be dealt with in accordance with these reasons. 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of New South Wales.
