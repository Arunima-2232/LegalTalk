CATCHWORDS 
 
Friend v Brooker 
 
Equity – Doctrine of contribution – "Co-ordinate liability"  – Appellant and 
respondent company directors – Respondent personally borrowed money which 
was on-lent to the company – Company ceased trading – Respondent sought 
funds from appellant to repay personal loan – Whether fiduciary relationship 
existed between the two – Whether co-ordinate liability existed so as to require 
equitable contribution from appellant. 
 
Words and phrases – "common design", "community of interest", "co-ordinate 
liability", "equitable contribution".
