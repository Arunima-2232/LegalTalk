CATCHWORDS 
 
Czatyrko v Edith Cowan University  
 
Negligence – Duty of care – Employer and employee –  Safe system of work – 
Suitable plant and equipment – Employee injured when attempting to stand on 
moveable platform while loading a truck – Platform not in position – Employee 
stepped backwards onto platform in belief it was raised – Absence of warning 
beeper – Absence of oral warning – Whether employer failed to devise and 
implement a safe system of work – Whether employer failed to provide suitable 
plant and equipment. 
 
Negligence – Contributory negligence – Employee injured when attempting to 
stand on moveable platform while loading a truck – Platform not in position – 
Employee stepped backwards onto platform in belief it was raised – Whether 
employee should have looked behind him before stepping backwards.
