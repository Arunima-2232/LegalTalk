ORDER 
 
1.  That part of the cause in proceedings No FUL 92 of 2000, pending in the Full 
Court of the Supreme Court of Western Australia which involves the following 
question: 
 
    "Are s 10 and s 7(2) of the Federal Courts (State Jurisdiction ) Act 1999 (WA)  
    invalid?" 
 
be removed into this Court pursuant to section 40 of the Judiciary Act 1903 (Cth) 
on the ground that such question arises under the Constitution and involves its 
interpretation. 
 
2.  The applicants pay the second respondent's costs of the application.
