M118/2001 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The question reserved for the decision of the Court be answered as follows: 
 
Question: "Did section 501(2) of the Migration Act  1958 (Cth) 
validly authorise the respondent to cancel the applicant's visa on 
27 June 2000?" 
 
Answer: "Yes".  
 
 The matter is remitted to a single Justice for further consideration  
and disposition. 
 
2. Costs of the case stated reserved to the Justice disposing of the cause.
