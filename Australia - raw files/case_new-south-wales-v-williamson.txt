damages" – Legal Profession Act 2004 (NSW), s 338 – Where "personal injury 
damages" defined to have same meaning as in Civil Liability Act 2002 (NSW), 
Pt 2 – Whether maximum costs limitation applies to claims for personal injury 
damages resulting from intentional acts.

operation of term in source statute – Effect of amendments to statute.
