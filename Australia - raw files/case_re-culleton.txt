ORDER 
 
1. The summons filed on 4 July 2018 is dismissed. 
 
2. Mr Culleton pay the Attorney -General of the Commonwealth's costs 
of and incidental to the summons. 
 
3. The costs of the summons be excluded from the order made by the 
Court on 3 February 2017 that Mr Culleton's costs of the proceeding 
be paid by the Commonwealth.
