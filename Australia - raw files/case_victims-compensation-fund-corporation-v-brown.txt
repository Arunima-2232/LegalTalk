ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside paragraphs 1 and 2 of the orders of the Court of Appeal of the 
Supreme Court of New South Wales made on 28 May 2002 and, in lieu 
thereof, order that: 
 
 (a) the record of the proceedings in the District Court be removed into 
the Court of Appeal; and  
 
 (b) the orders of Phelan DCJ made on 19 March 2001 in proceedings 
Nos 317/00 and 318/00 be quashed and, in lieu thereof, the 
determination of the Victims Compensation Tribunal made on 
16 May 2000 be affirmed.
