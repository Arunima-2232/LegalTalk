 
HIGH COURT OF AUSTRALIA

TRAVELEX LTD  APPELLANT

COMMISSIONER OF TAXATION RESPONDENT 
 
 
Travelex Ltd v Commissioner of Taxation [2010] HCA 33 
29 September 2010 
S79/2010 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Full Court of the Federal Court of Australia 
made on 29 September 2009, and in its place order that: 
 
 (a) the appeal to that Court be allowed with costs; and 
 
 (b) the order of Emmett J made on 19 December 2008 be set aside, and 
in its place: 
 
  (i) there be a declaration that the sale by Travelex Ltd of 
400 Fijian dollars to Geoffrey Urquhart on 25 November 
2007 on the departures side of the Customs barrier at 
Sydney International Airport was a supply of or in relation 
to rights and a GST-free supply by reason of item 4(a) of the 
table in s 38-190(1) of A New Tax System (Goods and 
Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth); and 
 
  (ii) it be ordered that the Commissioner of Taxation pay 
Travelex Ltd's costs of the proceedings. 
 
 
On appeal from the Federal Court of Australia
