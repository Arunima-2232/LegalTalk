Agricultural and Rural Finance Pty Limited v Gardiner   
[2008] HCA 57 
11 December 2008 
S180/2008 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside paragraphs 1-3, 7 and 8 of the order of the Court of Appeal of 
the Supreme Court of New South Wales made on 6 September 2007, and 
in place thereof order: 
 
(a) appeal allowed in part; 
 
(b) set aside paragraphs 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 of the order made by 
 Young CJ in Eq on 11 April 2006;  
 
(c) appellant have judgment in the sum of the amounts owing 
 under the relevant loan agreement for principal and interest on 
 the first, second and fourth loans ("the sum"); and 
 
(d) first respondent to pay the appellant' s costs in the Equity 
 Division of the Supreme Court of New South Wales and in the 
 appeal to the Court of Appeal.  
 
3. The parties have 21 days from the date of this order to agree upon the 
sum, and in default of agreement the matter be remitted to the Court of 
Appeal for determination of the sum.  
 
4. First respondent to pay the costs of the appellant and the second 
respondent in this Court.   
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of New South Wales
