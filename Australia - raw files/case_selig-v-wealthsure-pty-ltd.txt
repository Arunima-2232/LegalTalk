A25/2014 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed.  
 
2. Set aside or ders 1, 2, 3 and 7 of the orders of the Full Court of the 
Federal Court of Australia made on 30 May 2014 and, in their place, 
order that:  
 
(a) order 2 of the orders of Lander J made on 18 April 2013 be 
varied by substituting "$1,716,680" in place of "$1,7 60,512"; 
and 
 
(b) the appeal and cross-appeal be otherwise dismissed.  
 
3. Set aside orders 1 and 2 of the orders of the Full Court of the 
Federal Court of Australia made on 26 June 2014 and, in their 
place, order that QBE Insurance (Australia) Ltd pay t he costs of the 
respondents, Mr and Mrs Selig, of the appeal to that Court.  
 
4. QBE Insurance (Australia) Ltd pay the appellants' costs of the 
appeal to this Court.
