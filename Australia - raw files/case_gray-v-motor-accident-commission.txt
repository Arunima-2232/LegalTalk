Gray v Motor Accident Commission (A36/1997) [1998] HCA 70 
17 November 1998 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Full Court of the Supreme Court of South 
Australia.  In lieu thereof, order that the appeal to that Court be allowed 
with costs and there be a new trial on the issue of damages, other than 
aggravated and exemplary damages. 
 
3. The costs of the new trial be in the discretion of the judge at that trial.
