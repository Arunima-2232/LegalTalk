ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the orders made by the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court 
of New South Wales on 10 May 2024 and, in lieu thereof, order that: 
 
(a) the appeal be dismissed; and 
 
(b) the appellant pay the second and third respondents' costs of 
and incidental to the appeal. 
 
3. The first respondent pay the appellants' costs of and incidental to the 
appeal.
