CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Buckley v The Queen 
 
 
Criminal Law – Sentencing – Indefinite sentence – Appellant pleaded guilty to 
serious violent and sexual offences – Whether the sentencing judge observed the 
correct principles in exercising the power to impose an indefinite sentence. 
 
Criminal Law – Sentencing – Whether the sentencing judge made material errors 
of fact requiring reconsideration of the sentencing discretion. 
 
 
Penalties and Sentencing Act 1992 (Q), Pt 10.
