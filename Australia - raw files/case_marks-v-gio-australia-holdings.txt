CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Michael Marks & Ors v GIO Australia Holdings Limited & Ors 
 
Trade Practices – Misleading or deceptive conduct – Damages – Measure of 
damages – Whether analogous with damages in tort or contract – Distinction 
between "expectation" and "reliance" loss – Whether loss or damage suffered or 
likely to be suffered – Whether damage caused "by conduct of" defendant. 
 
Words and Phrases – "loss or damage", "injury", "likely to suffer". 
 
Trade Practices Act 1974 (Cth) – ss 4K, 52, 82, 87.
