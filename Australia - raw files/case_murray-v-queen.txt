ORDER 
 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of Queensland dated 24 August 
1999 and, in lieu thereof, order that: 
 
(a) the appellant's appeal to that Court be allowed; 
 
(b) the appellant's conviction be quashed and a new trial be had.

CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Murray v The Queen 
 
 
Criminal law – Homicide – Unlawful killing – Murder – Deceased died from gun 
shot wounds to chest – Whether shooting was an unwilled act or an event 
occurring by accident – What is "the act causing death" – Whether trial judge 
erred in failing to direct jury about unwilled acts –  Whether it is for the jury to 
decide what is "the act causing death" – Whether trial judge's failure to direct 
jury gave rise to a substantial miscarriage of justice so that a new trial should be 
ordered. 
 
Onus of proof – Whether the trial judge erred in directions to jury about onus of 
proof – Whether trial judge's direction to jury was apt to mislead the jury about 
the decision which was to be made. 
 
Words and phrases – "act" – "event" – "accident". 
 
The Criminal Code (Q), s 23.
