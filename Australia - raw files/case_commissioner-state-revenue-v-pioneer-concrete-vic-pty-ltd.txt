[2002] HCA 43

M13/2002 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside orders made by the Court of Appeal of Victoria on 2 May 2001 and, in 
lieu thereof, order that the appeal to that Court be dismissed with costs. 
 
3. Remit the matter to the Supreme Court of Victoria for consideration of the 
outstanding issues.
