CATCHWORDS 
 
Bruton Holdings Pty Limited (in liquidation) v Commissioner of Taxation 
 
Taxation – Recovery of tax debts – Company deposited moneys with third party 
– Passage of creditors' resolution for winding up of company – Commissioner of 
Taxation ("Commissioner") issued notice under s 260-5 in Sched 1 to the 
Taxation Administration Act 1953 (Cth) ("Administration Act") requiring 
moneys held by third party be paid to Commissioner – Whether s 260-5 notice 
may be issued after commencement of winding up – Whether steps taken by 
Commissioner void and unenf orceable as an "attachment" within meaning of 
s 500(1) of Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) –  Relationship between s 260-5 and 
s 500(1). 
 
Companies – Winding up – Creditors' voluntary winding up – Whether s 260-5 in 
Sched 1 to Administration Act applicable – Relationship between s 260-5 and 
s 260-45.  
 
Words and phrases – "attachment", "property".
