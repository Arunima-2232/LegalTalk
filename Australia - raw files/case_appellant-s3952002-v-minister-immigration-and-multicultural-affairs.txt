ORDER 
 
In each matter: 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Full Court of the Federal Court dated 
20 February 2002 and, in place thereof, order that: 
 
 (a) the appeal be allowed with costs; and  2. 
 
 (b) the orders of Lindgren J dated 26 July 2001 be set aside and, in place 
thereof, order that: 
 
  (i) the application be granted with costs; 
 
  (ii) the decision of the Refugee Review Tribunal dated 5 February 
2001 be set aside; and  
 
  (iii) the matter be remitted to the Tribunal for re-determination.
