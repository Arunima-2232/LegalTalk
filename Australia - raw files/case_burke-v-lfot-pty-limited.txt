CATCHWORDS 
 
Burke v LFOT Pty Limited 
 
Trade and commerce – Damages – Equitable contribution – Liability to pay 
damages under ss 75B, 82, 87 of Trade Practices Act 1974 (Cth) for breach of 
s 52 of the Act – Whether solicitor who gave negligent advice should contribute 
to the loss suffered by his client as a consequence of another's misrepresentation 
which loss could have been avoided by careful advice by the solicitor – Whether 
equitable maxims prevent requirement of contribution. 
 
Equity – Equitable contribution – Scope of – Requirement of co-ordinate liability 
– Whether solicitor who gave negligent advice should contribute to loss suffered 
by client as a consequence of another's representation where the loss could have 
been avoided by careful advice by the solicitor. 
 
Contribution – Equitable contribution – Scope of and availability – Co-ordinate 
liability – Requirements of – Whether compatible with the obligations imposed 
by Trade Practices Act 1974 (Cth) for breach of s 52 of the Act. 
 
Words and phrases – "co-ordinate liability", "natural justice". 
 
Trade Practices Act 1974 (Cth), ss 52, 75B, 82, 87.
