[2004] HCA 32 
17 June 2004 
A256/2003 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Orders of the Federal Court of Australia made on 11 March 2003, to the 
extent that they relate to the appeal to that Court from the orders of the 
Federal Magistrates Court made on 20 December 2002, set aside (save 
that as to costs).  In place thereof, order that the appeal to that Court 
against the orders of the Federal Magistrates Court is dismissed. 
 
3. Appellant to pay the respondent's costs in this Court. 
 
On appeal from the Federal Court of Australia
