Minister for Immigration and Citizenship v SZMDS [2010] HCA 16 
26 May 2010 
S193/2009 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The appeal be allowed. 
 
2. Orders 3, 4 and 5 made by the Federal Court of Australia on 10 March 
2009 be set aside. 
 
3. In place of those orders: 
 
 (a) the appeal to the Federal Court of Australia be dismissed; and 
 
 (b) Order 2 made by the Federal Magistrates Court of Australia on 
8 July 2008 be set aside. 
 
4. The appellant pay the reasonable costs of the first respondent of the 
appeal to this Court.
