CATCHWORDS 
 
King v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Appeal against conviction – Jury misdirection – Appellant 
convicted of two counts of "culpable driving causing death" contrary to s 318(1) 
of the Crimes Act 1958 (Vic) (" the Act ") – Jury had power under the Act to 
return an alternative verdict of " dangerous driving causing death " contrary to s 
319(1) if satisfied that accused not guilty of offence charged under s  318 – Trial 
judge directed jury that dangerous driving established by proof accused drove in 
way that "significantly increased the risk of harming others"  and that Crown did 
not have to show driving was "deserving of criminal punishment" – Whether trial 
judge misdirected jury – Whether R v De Montero (2009) 25 VR 694 should be 
followed – Whether departure from trial according to law or miscarriage of 
justice.  
 
Words and phrases – "culpable driving causing death", "deserving of criminal 
punishment", "dangerous driving causing death".  
 
Crimes Act 1958 (Vic), ss 318, 319, 422A(1).
