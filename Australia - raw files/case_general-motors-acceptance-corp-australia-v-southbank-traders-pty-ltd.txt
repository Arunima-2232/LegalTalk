General Motors Acceptance Corp Australia v Southbank Traders Pty Ltd 
[2007] HCA 19

ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Victoria made on 31 May 2006. 
 
3. Remit the matter to the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of Victoria 
for further consideration in accordance with the reasons of this Court. 
 
4. The Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of Victoria to determine the 
question of costs at trial and in the Court of Appeal in the light of its 
further disposition of the matter.
