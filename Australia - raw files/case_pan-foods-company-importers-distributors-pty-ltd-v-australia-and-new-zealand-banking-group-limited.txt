CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Pan Foods Company Importers & Distributors Pty Ltd v  
Australia and New Zealand Banking Group Limited  
 
 
Mortgage – Construction of terms – Mortgage agreement provided that event of 
default occurred if bank formed opinion that circumstances had arisen which had 
material adverse effect on business, assets and financial condition of borrower 
and ability of borrower to perform its obligations to the bank – Formation of 
requisite opinion – Declaration that moneys owing were immediately due and 
payable – Notice demanding payment – Validity of notice. 
 
Mortgage – Debenture – Default – Power in mortgage to appoint receiver after 
moneys became payable – Validity of appointment – Mortgage and loan 
agreements each speci fying circumstances in which moneys became payable –  
No inconsistency between mortgage and loan agreements.
