Esso Australia Resources Limited v The Commissioner of Taxation 
[1999] HCA 67 
21 December 1999 
M53/1999 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Full Court of the Federal Court of Australia 
made on 22 December 1998, and in place thereof, order that: 
 
   i) the appeal to that Court be allowed with costs; and 
 
  ii) the respondents pay the costs of the proceedings before Foster 
J. 
 
3. Questions of law raised for decision for Foster J answered: 
 
  a)   The correct test is the dominant purpose test, which is the 
common law test for claiming legal professional privilege. 
 
   b) Does not arise.
