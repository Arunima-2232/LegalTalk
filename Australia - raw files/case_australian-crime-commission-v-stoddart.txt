CATCHWORDS 
 
Australian Crime Commission v Stoddart 
 
Evidence – Privilege – Spousal privilege –  Witness summonsed pursuant to 
s 28(1) of Australian Crime Commission Act 2002 (Cth) ("Act") to give evidence 
regarding "federally relevant criminal activity" involving her husband – Witness 
declined to answer examiner's questions by claiming spousal privilege – Whether 
spousal privilege exists at common law and, if so, whether spousal privilege 
extends to non-curial proceedings – If spousal privilege exists at common law, 
whether Act restricts or abrogates spousal privilege.  
 
Words and phrases – "compellability", "competence", "spousal privilege".
