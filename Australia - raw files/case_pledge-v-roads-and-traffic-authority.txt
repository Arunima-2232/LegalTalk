Matters No. S122/2003, S123/2003, S124/2003 and S125/2003 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeals allowed with costs. 
 
2. Judgment and orders of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New 
South Wales on 10 April 2002 set aside. 
 
3. Matter remitted to the Court of Appeal to determine re-apportionment of 
liability among the Respondents to the second appeal and other matters that 
were before the Court of Appeal but not dealt with in its judgment including 
the matter of costs in that Court.
