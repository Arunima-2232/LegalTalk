Grajewski v Director of Public Prosecutions (NSW)  
[2019] HCA 8 
13 March 2019 
S141/2018 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2.  Set aside the order of  the Court of Criminal Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of New South Wales dated 24 October 2017 and in lieu thereof 
order that the questions submitted to the Court of Criminal Appeal 
by Judge Bright be answered as follows:  
 
Can these facts support a finding of guilt for an offence 
contrary to section 195(1)(a), Crimes Act, 1900?  
 
No.  
 
In particular, was the evidence capable of proving beyond 
reasonable doubt that Ship Loader 2 had been damaged by 
the conduct of Paul Olaf GRAJEWSKI?  
 
Unnecessary to answer.  
 
3. Quash the conviction and sentence of the District Court at Newcastle 
on 29 May 2017 on the appeal to the District Court.
