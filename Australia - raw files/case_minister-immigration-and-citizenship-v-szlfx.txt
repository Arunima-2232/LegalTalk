CATCHWORDS 
 
Minister for Immigration and Citizenship v SZLFX 
 
Immigration – Refugees – Review by Refugee Review Tribunal ("RRT") – 
Person telephoned, for purpose of obtaining information from that person, 
without procedures set out in ss 424(3) and 424B of Migration Act 1958 (Cth) 
("Act") being followed – Whether RRT breached ss 424(3) and 424B of Act. 
 
Immigration – Refugees – RRT did not give notice to first respondent of file note 
of conversation between RRT employee and third person – Whether RRT was 
required by s 424A of Act to give notice – Whether file note was "the reason, or 
a part of the reason, for affirming the decision that is under review". 
 
Words and phrases – "get any information", "invite", "reason, or a part of the 
reason". 
 
Migration Act 1958 (Cth), Pt 7 Div 4, ss 424, 424A, 424B.
