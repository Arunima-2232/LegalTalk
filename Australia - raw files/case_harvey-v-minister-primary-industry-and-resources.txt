Harvey v Minister for Primary Industry and Resources 
[2024] HCA 1 
Date of Hearing: 5 September 2023 
Date of Judgment: 7 February 2024 
D9/2022 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside order 1 of the orders made by the Full Court  of the Federal 
Court of Australia on 29 April 2022 and in its place: 
 
(a) order that the appeal be allowed; 
 
(b) declare that the grant of ML 29881 under s 40(1)(b)(ii) of the 
Mineral Titles Act 2010 (NT) is a future act that is the creation 
of a right to mine for the sole purpose of the construction of an 
infrastructure faci lity associated with mining within 
s 24MD(6B)(b) of the Native Title Act 1993 (Cth); and 
 
(c) order that the first respondent be restrained from deciding the 
application for ML 29881 until completion of the procedures in 
s 24MD(6B) of the Native Title Act 1993 (Cth). 
 
3. Each party bear its own costs of the appeal.
