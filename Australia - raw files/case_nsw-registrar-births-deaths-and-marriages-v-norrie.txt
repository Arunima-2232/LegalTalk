NSW Registrar of Births, Deaths and Marriages v Norrie 
[2014] HCA 11 
2 April 2014 
S273/2013 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Set aside paragraph 3(b) of the order of the Court of Appeal of the 
Supreme Court of New South Wales made on 14 June 2013 and, in 
its place, order that the respondent's applications dated 
26 November 2009 be remitted to the NSW Registrar of Births, 
Deaths and Marriages for determination in accordance with the 
reasons of this Court. 
 
2. Appeal otherwise dismissed. 
 
3. Appellant to pay the respondent's costs of the appeal to this Court.
