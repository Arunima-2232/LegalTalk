CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Kennedy Cleaning Services Pty Limited v Petkoska 
 
Workers' compensation (ACT) – Injury – Personal injury arising in the course of 
employment – Employee had diseased heart valve and suffered a lesion caused 
by a blood clot which resulted in a stroke – Whether mutually exclusive statutory 
regimes for "injury" and "disease". 
 
Words and phrases – "injury" – "disease" – "lesion". 
 
Workers' Compensation Act 1951 (ACT), ss 6(1), 7(1), 9, 9A, 9B. 
Workers Compensation Act 1987 (NSW), s 4.
