CATCHWORDS 
 
Wallace v Kam 
 
Negligence – Causation – Medical practitioner – Where medical practitioner 
failed to warn patient of two distinct material risks inherent in surgical procedure 
– Where only one risk eventuated – Where patient would have chosen not to 
undergo surgical procedure if warned of both risks – Where patient would have 
chosen to undergo surgical procedure if warned only of risk that eventuated – 
Whether failure to warn of both material risks was a necessary condition of 
injury caused by the risk that eventuated – Whether appropriate for scope of 
medical practitioner's liability to extend to that injury. 
 
Words and phrases – "but for", "factual causation", "scope of liability". 
 
Civil Liability Act 2002 (NSW), s 5D.
