ORDER 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Special leave to cross-appeal refused. 
 
3. Appellant to pay the costs of the respondent in this Court. 
 
4. Set aside the orders of the Court of Appeal of Victoria made on 4 December 
1998 and in place thereof order that: 
 
 i) the appeal to the Court of Appeal of Victoria be allowed; 
 
 ii) the order of the County Court of Victoria made on 30 June 1998 be set 
aside; and 
 
 iii) there be judgment for the appellant in the sum of $41,699.22.
