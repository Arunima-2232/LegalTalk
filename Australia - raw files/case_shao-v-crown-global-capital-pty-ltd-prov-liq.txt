Date of Hearing: 2 September 2025 
Date of Judgment: 5 November 2025 
S46/2025 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders made by the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court 
of New South Wales on 19 December 2024 and in their place it be 
ordered that: 
 
(a) the appeal be allowed with costs; 
 
(b) the orders made by the Supreme Court of New South Wales on 
14 July 2023 be set aside and in their place it be ordered that: 
 
(i) judgment be entered for the plaintiff against the 
defendants in the sum of $1,133,117.40, together with 
interest pursuant to s  100 of the Civil Procedure Act 
2005 (NSW) in the sum of $431,048.82; and 
 
(ii) the defendants pay the plaintiff's costs of the 
proceedings. 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of New South Wales
