CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Risk v Northern Territory of Australia 
 
 
Aboriginals – Land rights – Claim under the Aboriginal Land Rights (Northern 
Territory) Act 1976 (Cth) to areas of the seabed of bays and gulfs within the 
limits of the Northern Territory – Whether the seabed of bays and gulfs amenable 
to claim – Whether "land in the Northern Territory" includes the seabed of bays 
or gulfs. 
 
Words and phrases – "land" – "land in the Northern Territory". 
 
Aboriginal Land Rights (Northern Territory) Act 1976 (Cth), s 3(1), definition of 
"Crown Land".
