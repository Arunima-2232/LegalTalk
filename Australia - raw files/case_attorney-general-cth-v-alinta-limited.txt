Attorney-General (Cth) v Alinta Limited  
[2008] HCA 2 
Date of order:  13 December 2007 
Date of publication of reasons:  31 January 2008 
S331/2007 
 
ORDER 
 
1.  Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside so much of paragraph 2 of the orders of the Full Court of the 
Federal Court of Australia made on 30  April 2007 as ordered that there 
be a declaration that s  657A(2)(b) of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) is 
invalid and, in its place, order that there be a declaration that 
s 657A(2)(b) of the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) is not invalid on the 
ground that it purports to confer the judicial power of the Commonwealth 
on the Takeovers Panel.
