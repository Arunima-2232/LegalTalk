CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Commissioner of Taxation v Ryan 
 
 
Income tax – Assessment – Notice stating no tax payable – Subsequent 
assessment showing assessable income and tax payable thereon – Whether initial 
notice an "assessment" for the purposes of s 170(3) of the Income Tax 
Assessment Act 1936 (Cth) – Whether subsequent assessment therefore 
unauthorised – Whether any tax became "due and payable" upon issue of initial 
notice. 
 
Words and phrases – "assessment" – "due and payable". 
 
Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (Cth), ss 6(1), 166, 170, 171, 204.
