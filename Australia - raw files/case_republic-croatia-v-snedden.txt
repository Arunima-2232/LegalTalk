CATCHWORDS 
 
Republic of Croatia v Snedden 
 
Extradition – Eligibility for extradition from Australia – Extradition objections – 
Whether objection established on basis that on surrender the respondent might be 
"punished, detained or restricted" in liberty "by reason of" his "political 
opinions" – Where respondent claimed prior service in Croatian armed forces 
was treated by Croatian courts as mitigating factor in sentencing – Where 
respondent alleged to have served with forces other than Croatian armed forces – 
Whether respondent's ineligibility for mitigating factor in sentencing was 
punishment – Whether such punishment was "by reason of" respondent's 
"political opinions". 
 
Words and phrases – "by reason of", "political opinions", "punished". 
 
Extradition Act 1988 (Cth), ss 7(c), 19(2)(d).
