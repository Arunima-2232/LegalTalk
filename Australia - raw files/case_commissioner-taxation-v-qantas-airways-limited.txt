CATCHWORDS  
 
Commissioner of Taxation v Qantas Airways Limited 
 
Goods and Services Tax – Taxable supply – Supply – Consideration – 
Overbooking – Attribution of tax period – Airfares that were non-refundable or 
refundable but unclaimed – Customer cancels or fails to take purchased flight –
Promise by airline to use best endeavours to carry passengers and baggage –  
Whether a taxable supply under A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act  
1999 (Cth), s 9-5 – Whether airline liable to remit to Commissioner GST on non-
refundable or unclaimed refundable fares. 
 
Words and phrases – "a supply for consideration", "consideration", "taxable 
supply".  
 
A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth), ss 7-1, 9-5, 9- 10, 
9-15, 29-5(1).
