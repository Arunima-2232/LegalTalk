S164/2010 
 
ORDER 
 
Order that the questions stated in the special case be answered as follows: 
 
Question 1: Is the Crimes (Criminal Organisations Control) Act 2009 
(NSW) or any provision or part of it invalid on the grounds 
that: 
 
a. it undermines the institutional integrity of the 
Supreme Court of New South Wales; or 
 
b. it is outside the legislative powers of the 
Parliament of the defendant? 
 
Answer: The Crimes (Criminal Organisations Control) Act 2009 
(NSW) is invalid. 
 
Question 2: Who should pay the costs of the special case and/or of the 
proceedings? 
 
Answer: The defendant is to pay the costs of the plaintiff of the 
special case.
