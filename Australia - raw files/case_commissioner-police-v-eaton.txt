Commissioner of Police v Eaton 
[2013] HCA 2 
8 February 2013 
S230/2012 
 
ORDER 
 
1.  Appeal allowed.  
 
2.  Set aside orders (a), (b) and (c) of the Court of Appeal of the 
Supreme Court of New South Wales made on 6 March 2012 and, in 
their place, order that the appeal to the Court of Appeal be 
dismissed.  
 
3. Set aside the orders of the Full Bench of the Industrial Relations 
Commission of New South Wales made on 24 March 2012.  
 
4. The appellant pay the first respondent's costs of the proceedings in 
this Court.
