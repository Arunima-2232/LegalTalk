ORDER 
 
1. Appeal dismissed with costs. 
 
2. Special leave to cross-appeal granted. 
 
3. Cross-appeal allowed with costs. 
 
4. Vary the judgment of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
South Australia dated 22 February 2024 by: 
 
(a) deleting the answer "Yes" to Question 3 of the questions of law 
referred for its consideration and substituting the answer "No"; 
and 
 
(b) replacing Order 1 of the order for costs with an order that "The 
appellant is to pay the respondent's costs".
