CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Thompson v His Honour Judge Byrne & Ors 
 
Criminal law – Motor traffic offence – Prescribed concentration of alcohol – 
Breath samples furnished within 3 hours after driving exceeded prescribed limit – 
Later blood sample showing concentration of alcohol within prescribed limit – 
Whether offence committed – Proper interpretation of s 49(1)(f) of the Road Safety 
Act 1986 (Vic). 
 
Precedent – Stare decisis – High Court – Whether Mills v Meeking (1990) 169 
CLR 214 should be reconsidered.
