M111/2020 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The grant of special leave to appeal on the second ground be revoked 
with costs.  
 
2.  The appeal be dismissed. 
 
3.  Special leave to cross -appeal be granted a nd the cross -appeals be 
allowed.  
 
4.  Set aside order 2 of the orders of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of Victoria made on 30 April 2020 and the orders of that Court 
made on 5 May 2020 and, in lieu of those orders, order that: 
 
(a)  the appeal be allowed;  
 
(b)  order 2 of the orders of the Supreme Court of Victoria made on 
20 December 2018 be  varied to replace the sum of 
$5,900,227.92 with the sum of $8,045,765.34 and the sum  of 
$3,101,822.56 with the sum of $4,229,758.04; 
   
 
  2. 
 
 
(c)  order 4 of the orders of the Supreme Court of Victoria made on 
20 December 2018 be  varied to replace the sum of 
$5,892,069.06 with the sum of $8,034,639.62 and the sum  of 
$3,084,619.22 with the sum of $4,223,909.11; and 
 
(d)  the fourth respondent pay the appellan ts' costs of the 
application for leave to appeal  and the appeal, to be taxed in 
default of agreement on the standard basis. 
  
5.  The appellant pay the costs of the appeal and the cross-appeals.
