CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Firebelt Pty Ltd v Brambles Australia Ltd 
 
 
Patents – Petty Patent – Revocation – Inventive step or obviousness –   
Combination patents – Proper construction of ss 7(2) and 7(3) of the Patents Act 
1990 (Cth) – Whether evidence supported conclusion that it had been shown to 
be obvious to place known integers in the interactive combination claimed in the 
Petty Patent –  Whether trial judge and Full Court, in focusing upon the 
incorporation of particular integers, gave inadequate weight to whether the 
selection in combination of the integers was obvious at the priority date – 
Relevance of commercial success of Petty Patent to question of obviousness. 
 
Patents Act 1990 (Cth), ss 7(2), 7(3).
