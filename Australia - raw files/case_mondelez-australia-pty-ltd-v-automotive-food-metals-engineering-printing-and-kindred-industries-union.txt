ORDER 
 
In each matter:  
 
1. Appeal allowed.  
 
2. Set aside order 1 made by the Full Court of the Federal Court of 
Australia on 21 August 2019 and in its place declare that: 
 
"The expression '10 days' in s 96(1) of the Fair Work Act 2009 
(Cth) means an amount of paid personal/carer's leave accruing 
for every year of service equivalent to an employee's ordinary 
hours of work in a week over a two -week (fortnightly) period, 
or 1/26 of the employee's ordinary hours of work in a year. 
A 'day' for the purposes of s  96(1) refers to a 'notional day', 
consisting of one -tenth of the equivalent of an employee's 
ordinary hours of work in a two-week (fortnightly) period."
