Minister for Immigration and Citizenship v Kumar 
 
Administrative law – Procedural fairness – Migration Review Tribunal 
("Tribunal") – Application for spousal visas – Migration Act 1958 (Cth) ss 5, 
357A, 359A – Definition of " non-disclosable information" – "Non-disclosable 
information" whose disclosure would found an action for breach of confidence – 
Relevance of "iniquity defence" – Whether Tribunal obliged to disclose to

Equity – Breach of confidence – Interaction of public and private law.
