Minister for Immigration and Border Protection v Kumar 
[2017] HCA 11 
8 March 2017 
P49/2016 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside order s 2-4 of the orders of the Federal Court of Australia 
made on 23 February 2016 and, in their place, order that:  
 
 (a) order 2 of the orders of the Federal Circuit Court of Australia 
made on 14 September 2015 be set aside; and 
 
 (b) the appeal to that Court otherwise be dismissed. 
 
3. The appellant pay the first, second and third respondents' costs of 
the appeal to this Court.
