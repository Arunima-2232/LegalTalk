Blackadder v Ramsey Butchering Services Pty Ltd 
[2005] HCA 22 
27 April 2005 
S186/2004 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed; 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Full Court of the Federal Court of Australia 
dated 21 February 2003 and, in their place, order that: 
 
 a. the appeal be allowed in part; 
 
 b. order 3 of the orders of Madgwick J in the Federal Court of Australia 
dated 21 May 2002 be varied by deleting therefrom the words "and for a 
period of 14 days thereafter"; 
 
 c. order 4 of those orders be set aside; and 
 
 d. otherwise the appeal be dismissed.
