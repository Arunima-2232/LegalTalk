CATCHWORDS 
 
 
MSP Nominees Pty Ltd v Commissioner of Stamps 
 
Stamp duties – Conveyance – Redemption of units in unit trust scheme – Register 
of units recorded cancellation – Whether register an instrument evidencing a 
deemed conveyance – Meaning of "transfer" in s 71(15) of the Stamp Duties Act 
1923 (SA) – Whether beneficiary surrendered or renounced beneficial interest in, 
or in relation to, property upon redemption of units. 
 
Trusts – Unit trust – Provision for redemption of units – Whether redemption a 
surrender, release or renunciation by unit holder of beneficial interest. 
 
Words and phrases – "transfer" – "surrender" – "renounce" – "release". 
 
Stamp Duties Act 1923 (SA), ss 24(4), 71.
