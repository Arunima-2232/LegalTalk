CATCHWORDS 
 
BCM v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Appeal – Appeal against conviction – Whether verdict 
unreasonable or unsupported having regard to evidence – Indecent dealing with 
child under 12 years – Inconsistencies in evidence of child complainant – 
Whether inconsistencies affected reliability – Whether inconsistencies went to 
essential features of complainant's account of offences.  
 
Criminal law – Reasons – Whether Court of Appeal gave sufficient reasons – 
Whether Court of Appeal's reasons disclosed assessment of capacity of evidence 
to support verdict – Obligation to give reasons not discharged by observation that 
jury entitled to accept evidence of complainant.  
 
Words and phrases – "unsafe and unsatisfactory", "verdict cannot be supported 
having regard to the evidence", "verdict is unreasonable". 
 
Criminal Code (Q), ss 210, 668E(1).
