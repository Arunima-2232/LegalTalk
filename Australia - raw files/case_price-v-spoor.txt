CATCHWORDS 
 
Price v Spoor  
 
Limitation of actions  – Exclusion by agreement – Where mortgages over land 
secured loan – Where mortgagors failed to repay loan – Where mortgagees brought 
proceedings to recover monies owing and possession of land secured by 
mortgages – Where mortgagors contended mortgagees stat ute-barred from 
enforcing rights under mortgages as a result of expiry of relevant time period under 
Limitation of Actions Act 1974 (Qld) ("Act") – Where mortgagors contended 
mortgagees' title under mortgages extinguished by operation of s 24 of Act – 
Where mortgagees contended that mortgagors agreed not to plead any defence 
under Act by virtue of cl 24 of mortgages – Whether cl 24 effective to prevent 
mortgagors from pleading any defence under Act – Whether agreement not to 
plead any defence under Act unenforceable as contrary to public policy – Whether 
s 24 of Act operated automatically to extinguish mortgagees' title at expiry of 
relevant time period – Whether mortgagees' remedy confined to damages for 
mortgagors' breach of cl 24 of mortgages.
