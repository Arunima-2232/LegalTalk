CATCHWORDS 
 
QLN147 v The Republic of Nauru 
 
Nauru – Appeal as of right from Supreme Court of Nauru – Refugees – Where 
Secretary of Department of Justice and Border Control refused applicati on for 
complementary protection – Where Refugee Status Review Tribunal affirmed 
Secretary's decision – Where appellant claimed he would be subject to cruel, 
inhuman or degrading treatment if returned to Sri Lanka – Where basis for claim 
was that appellant may be remanded in prison if returned to Sri Lanka and prison 
conditions in Sri Lanka are poor – Whether Tribunal had regard to material 
before it concerning prison conditions in Sri Lanka – Whether reasons of 
Tribunal met standard required by s 34(4) of Refugees Convention Act 2012 (Nr). 
 
Words and phrases – "cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment", "duty to give 
reasons", "prison conditions". 
 
Refugees Convention Act 2012 (Nr), ss 5(1), 6(1), 34(4).
