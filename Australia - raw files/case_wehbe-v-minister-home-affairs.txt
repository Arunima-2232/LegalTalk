HIGH COURT OF AUSTRALIA 
 
EDELMAN J 
 
 
 
SARA WEHBE PLAINTIFF 
 
AND 
 
MINISTER FOR HOME AFFAIRS DEFENDANT 
 
 
Wehbe v Minister for Home Affairs 
[2018] HCA 50 
7 November 2018 
S217/2018 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The plaintiff's application for an order extending time under 
s 486A(2) of the Migration Act 1958 (Cth) be refused. 
 
2. The application for an order to show cause filed on 21 August 2018 
be dismissed. 
 
3. The plaintiff pay the defendant's costs.
