ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Cri minal Appeal of the Northern 
Territory made on 19 December 2014 and, in its place, order that: 
 
(a) the appeal be allowed; 
 
(b) the appellant's conviction on counts 2, 3 and 4 of the 
indictment be quashed; and 
 
(c) a new trial be had on counts 2, 3 and 4 of the indictment.
