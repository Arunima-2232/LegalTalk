ORDER 
 
In Matter No S79 of 2022: 
 
1.  The appeal be allowed with costs. 
 
2. Orders 2 to 6 of  the orders made by the Full Court  of the Federal 
Court of Australia on 7 September  2021 and the declarations and  
orders made by the Full Court of the Federal Court  of Australia on 
13 October 2021 in proceeding NSD 35 of 2021 be set aside and, in  
their place, order that the appeal be dismissed with costs. 
 
 
In Matter No S80 of 2022: 
 
1.  The appeal be allowed with costs. 
 
2.  The orders made by the Full  Court of the Federal Court of Australia 
on 7 September 2021 in proceeding NSD 249 of 2021 be set aside and, 
in their place, order that the appeal be dismissed with costs.
