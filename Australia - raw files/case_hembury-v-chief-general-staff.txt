Hembury v Chief of the General Staff (M102/1997) [1998] HCA 47 
23 July 1998 
 
ORDER 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Federal Court of Australia and in lieu thereof 
order that the appeal to that Court be allowed, the convictions quashed 
and the matter returned to the Defence Force Discipline Appeal 
Tribunal for the making of such orders as are appropriate conformably 
with the decision of this Court. 
 
 
 
 
On appeal from the Federal Court of Australia
