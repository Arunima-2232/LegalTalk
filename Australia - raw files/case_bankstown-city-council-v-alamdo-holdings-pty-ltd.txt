Bankstown City Council v Alamdo Holdings Pty Ltd 
[2005] HCA 46 
7 September 2005 
S480/2004 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside orders 1 and 2 of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
New South Wales entered on 12 October 2004 and in their place order: 
 
 (a) the appeal to that Court is allowed; and 
 
 (b) orders 1 - 5 of Gzell J made on 16 December 2003 are set aside 
and in their place order that the proceedings be dismissed. 
 
3. Appellant to pay the costs of the respondent of the appeal to this Court. 
 
4. Respondent's application for special leave to appeal is dismissed with 
costs. 
 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of New South Wales
