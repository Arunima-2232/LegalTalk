CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Stanoevski v The Queen 
 
 
Evidence – Evidence Act 1995 (NSW) – Character evidence – Accused raised 
own good character –  Judicial discretion to allow cross-examination of accused 
on alleged past misdeeds not directly related to facts in issue – Whether 
discretion to allow cross-examination miscarried. 
 
Words and phrases –  "good character" – "credibility" – "leave, permission or 
direction" – "unfairness". 
 
Evidence Act 1995 (NSW), ss 55, 56, 102, 104, 106, 112, 135, 192. 
Legal Profession Act 1987 (NSW), s 155.
