CATCHWORDS 
 
Re Golding  
 
High Court – Appellate jurisdiction  – Application for special leave to appeal  – 
Abuse of process – Where applicant applied for special leave to appeal – Where 
applicant previously made application for special leave to appeal – Where previous 
application for special leave refused – Where substance of subsequent application 
for special leave substantially identical to substance of previous application for 
special leave – Where no exceptional circumstances identified in subsequent 
application explaining failure to raise novel issues in previous application – 
Whether subsequent application for special leave an abuse of process. 
 
Words and phrases – "abuse of process", "application for special leave to appeal", 
"compelling explanation or circumstance", "exceptional circumstance", 
"interlocutory application", "res judicata", "same subject matter", "special leave".
