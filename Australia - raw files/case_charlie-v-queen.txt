CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Charlie v The Queen 
 
Criminal law – Murder – Relationship between general Code provision 
establishing excuse from criminal responsibility and specific provision creating 
offence of murder –  Whether accused must foresee death as a possible 
consequence of conduct – Principles of statutory construction – Relevance of 
common law in construction of Code. 
 
Criminal Code (NT), ss 31, 162(1)(a).
