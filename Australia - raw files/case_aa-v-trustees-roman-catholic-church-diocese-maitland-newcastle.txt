ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside orders 1 and 2 made by the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New South Wales on 15 April 2025 and orders 7 and 8 made by the Court of Appeal on 7 May 2025. In their place, order that the appeal to the Court of Appeal be dismissed with costs save to the extent that order 1 of the orders made by Schmidt A-J on 18 October 2024 be varied to substitute for "$636,480.00" the sum of $335,960.
