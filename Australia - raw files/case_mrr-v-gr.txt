ORDER 
 
1. The appeal from the whole of the judgment and orders of the Full Court of 
the Family Court of Australia given and made on 15 May 2009 be 
allowed. 
 
2. The orders of the Full Court of the Family Court of Australia made on 
15 May 2009 be set aside and in their place order that: 
 
 (a) the appeal by the mother against the orders of the Federal 
Magistrates Court of Australia made on 1 April 2008 be allowed; 
 
 (b) the orders of the Federal Magistrates Court of Australia made on 
1 April 2008 be set aside;  
 
 (c) the matter be remitted to the Federal Magistrates Court of 
Australia for rehearing de novo. 
 
 
On appeal from the Family Court of Australia
