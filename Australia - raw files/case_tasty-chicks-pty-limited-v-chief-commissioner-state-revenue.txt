Tasty Chicks Pty Limited v Chief Commissioner of State Revenue  
[2011] HCA 41 
5 October 2011 
S218/2011 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed.  
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New 
South Wales dated 4 January 2011. 
 
3. Remit the matter to the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New South 
Wales for further hearing in accordance with the reasons for judgment of 
this Court.  
 
4. The costs to date in the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New South 
Wales be determined by that Court on its final disposition of the appeal.   
 
5. The respondent pay the appellants' costs in this Court.
