The Queen v Nguyen [2010] HCA 38 
3 November 2010 
M23/2010 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Special leave to appeal granted. 
 
2. Special leave to cross-appeal granted. 
 
3.  Appeal and cross-appeal each treated as instituted and heard instanter 
and allowed.  
 
4. Set aside the orders of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Victoria made in respect of the respondent on 18 December 2009, and, in 
their place, order that: 
 
(a) leave to appeal to that Court against conviction be granted;  
 
(b) the appeal to that Court be allowed;  
 
(c) the respondent's convictions be quashed; and 
 
(d) a new trial be had. 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of Victoria
