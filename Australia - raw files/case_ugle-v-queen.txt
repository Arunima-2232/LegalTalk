Ugle v The Queen [2002] HCA 25 
20 June 2002 
P61/2001 
 
ORDER 
 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Criminal Appeal of Western Australia 
dated 15 March 2001 and, in lieu thereof, order that: 
 
(a) the appellant's appeal to that Court be allowed; 
 
(b) the appellant's conviction be quashed and a new trial be had. 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of Western Australia
