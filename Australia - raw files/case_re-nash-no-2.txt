ORDER 
 
1. The summons filed on 7 November 2017 for a declaration that 
Ms Hollie Hughes is duly elected as a senator for the State of New 
South Wales for the place for which Ms Fiona Nash was returned is 
dismissed.  
 
2. The Commonwealth is to pay Ms Hughes' costs.
