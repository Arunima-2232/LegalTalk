ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs.   
 
2. Set aside orders 2 and 3 of the orders of the Court of Appeal of the 
Supreme Court of Queensland made on 12 December 2003 and in their 
place order that judgment be entered for the appellant in the sum of 
$105,327.92.  
 
3. Respondent to pay the appellant's costs of the proceedings in the District 
Court of Queensland.

CATCHWORDS 
 
Thompson v Woolworths (Q'land) Pty Limited 
 
Negligence – Duty of care – Independent contractor delivering goods in pursuit 
of mutual commercial purpose – Delivery person suffered back injury attempting 
to move industrial waste bins blocking supermarket loading dock – Content of 
duty to exercise reasonable care for safety of entrants – Consideration of aspects 
of relationship between occupier and entrant.   
 
Negligence – Contributory negligence – Independent contractor – Relevance of 
failure to wait for assistance before attempting to move bins – Relevance of 
knowledge of previous injury.
