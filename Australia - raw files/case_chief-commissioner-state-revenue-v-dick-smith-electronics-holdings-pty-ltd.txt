ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New 
South Wales entered on 7 November 2003, and in its place order that:

place order that: 
 
 (i) The assessment made by the defendant on or about 13 July 
2001 is confirmed; 
 
 (ii) The plaintiff pay the defendant's costs.
