Azzopardi v The Queen [2001] HCA 25 
3 May 2001 
S105/2000 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Court of Criminal Appeal of New South Wales 
dated 1 October 1998 and in lieu thereof order that: 
 
a) the appellant’s appeal to that Court be allowed; 
 
b) the appellant’s conviction be set aside; and  
 
c) there be a new trial.
