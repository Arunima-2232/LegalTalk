CATCHWORDS 
 
Dinsdale v The Queen 
 
 
Criminal law – Sentencing – Crown appeals against sentence – Circumstances in 
which Court of Criminal Appeal should allow appeal against sentence and 
substitute its own sentence – Necessity to find and identify error before allowing 
appeal – Whether failure to do so in reasons of Court of Criminal Appeal. 
 
Criminal law – Sentencing – Crown appeals against sentence – Circumstances in 
which judicial discretion to suspend sentence should be exercised – Whether 
power to suspend sentence is confined by reference wholly, mainly or specially 
to the effect on rehabilitation of the offender. 
 
Criminal Code (WA), s 688. 
Sentencing Act 1995 (WA), ss 39(2), 76.
