CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Allan v Transurban City Link Limited 
 
 
Administrative law – Judicial review – Standing – Legislation providing review 
process – Under legislation application for review may be made by a "person 
who is affected by a reviewable decision" – Whether applicant is such a person is 
to be determined by construction of the legislation not by general considerations 
respecting "standing".  
 
Administrative law –  Judicial review – Reviewable decision –  Legislation 
establishing tax incentive scheme by issue of tax certificates in respect of 
"infrastructure borrowings" – Issue of tax certificate – Whether application for 
review may be brought in respect of a decision to issue a tax certificate. 
 
Words and phrases –  "reviewable decision", "person who is affected by a 
reviewable decision". 
 
Development Allowance Authority Act 1992 (Cth), ss 93A-93ZG, 93AA, 119, 
120. 
Administrative Appeals Tribunal Act 1975 (Cth), ss 25, 27. 
Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (Cth), ss 159GZZZZD-159GZZZZH. 
Melbourne City Link Act 1995 (Vic).
