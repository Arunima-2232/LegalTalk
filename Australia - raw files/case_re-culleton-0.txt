ORDER 
 
1. The summons filed by Senator Culleton on 12 January 2017 is 
dismissed. 
 
2. The costs of the summons are excluded from such order as may be 
made to the effect that Senator Culleton's costs of the proceeding on 
the reference be paid by the Commonwealth.
