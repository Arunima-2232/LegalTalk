Honeysett v The Queen 
[2014] HCA 29 
13 August 2014 
S57/2014 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Criminal Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of New South Wales made on 5 June 2013 and, in its place, 
order that: 
 
(a)  the appeal be allowed; 
 
(b) the appellant's conviction be quashed; and 
 
(c)  a new trial be had.
