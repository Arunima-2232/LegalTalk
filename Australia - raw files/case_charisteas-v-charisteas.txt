ORDER 
 
1. The first respondent's application for an extension of time to file a 
notice of contention be refused.  
 
2. The appeal be allowed with the first respondent to pay the costs of the 
appeal.  
 
3.  The orders of the Full Court of the Family Court of Australia made on 
10 July 2020 be set aside and, in their place, there be orders that: 
 
(a)  the husband's appeal be allowed with the wife to pay the costs 
of the appeal, including any costs of the wife's cross-appeal;  
 
(b)  the orders made by Walters J on 10 February 2015 and 
12 February 2018 be set aside; and  
 
(c) the application for orders pursuant to s 79 of the Family Law 
Act 1975 (Cth) further or in addition to paragraphs 1, 5-13 and 
15 of the orders made by Crisford J on 9 Dec ember 2011 be 
remitted to the Family Court of Western Australia for 
rehearing.
