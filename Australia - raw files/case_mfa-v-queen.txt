CATCHWORDS 
 
MFA v The Queen 
 
Criminal law –  Appeal – Indictment containing multiple counts of  sexual 
offences with respect to one complainant – Verdicts of guilty on two counts and 
acquittals on the remaining counts – Whether verdicts unreasonable –  
Significance of acquittals when considering unreasonableness of guilty verdicts –  
Test for determination of unreasonableness of jury's verdict – Significance of 
disparities in evidence and failure of prosecution to call witness. 
 
Criminal law and practice – Court of Criminal Appeal –  Whether error shown in 
Court of Criminal Appeal's reasons –  Whether proceedings should be returned to 
that Court – Whether High Court should perform appellate reconsideration. 
 
Word and phrases – "unreasonable, or cannot be supported, having regard to the 
evidence". 
 
Criminal Appeal Act 1912 (NSW), s 6(1).
