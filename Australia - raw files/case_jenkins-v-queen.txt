CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Jenkins v The Queen 
 
 
Criminal law – Direction to jury – Accomplice warning – Prosecution witness 
pleaded guilty to criminal offences related to charges against appellant – 
Testimony of witness substantially undisputed – Defence did not seek to attack 
credit of witness – Defence case relied in part on evidence of witness – No 
warning sought at trial as to reliability of witness – Whether trial judge obliged to 
warn jury that it would be dangerous to convict on uncorroborated evidence of an 
accomplice – Whether trial judge obliged to warn jury that evidence was 
potentially unreliable and must be subjected to careful scrutiny.
