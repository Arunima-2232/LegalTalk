CATCHWORDS 
 
Roadshow Films Pty Ltd v iiNet Limited 
 
Intellectual property –  Copyright – Infringement – Authorisation – Appellants 
owners and exclusive licensees of copyright in commercially released films and 
television programs ("appellants' films") – Respondent internet service provider 
supplied internet services under agreement requiring that services not be used to 
infringe others' rights or for illegal purposes – Users of respondent's internet 
services infringed copyright in appellants' films by making appellants' films 
available online using BitTorrent peer- to-peer file sharing system – Notices 
served on respondent alleging copyright infringement by users of respondent's 
internet services – Respondent took no action in response to notices – Whether 
respondent authorised infringement of copyright in appellants' films by users of 
respondent's internet services. 
 
Words and phrases – "authorise". 
 
Copyright Act 1968 (Cth), ss 86(c), 101(1), 101(1A).
