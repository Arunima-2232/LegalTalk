The Republic of Nauru v WET040 [No 2] 
[2018] HCA 60 
5 December 2018 
M154/2017 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Su preme Court of Nauru made on 
28 September 2017 and, in their place, order that the appeal to that 
Court be dismissed with costs. 
 
3. The respondent pay the appellant's costs of this appeal. 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of Nauru
