ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Federal Court of Australia made on 
14 August 2012 and, in their place, order that: 
 
(a) the appeal from the Federal Magistrates Court of Australia to 
the Federal Court be allowed;  
 
(b) the order of the Federal Magistrates Court made on 
20 October 2011 be set aside; and 
 
(c) the application be remitted to the Federal Circuit Court of 
Australia to be heard and determined according to law. 
 
 
On appeal from the Federal Court of Australia
