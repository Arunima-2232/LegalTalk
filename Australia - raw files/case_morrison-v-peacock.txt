ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside order of the New South Wales Court of Criminal Appeal made on 
30 October 2000 in so far as it ordered that question (i)(a) of the questions 
reserved be answered "Yes", and in its place order that question (i)(a) be 
answered as follows: 
 
Question (i)(a): 
 
As a matter of law can the wear and tear in consequence of which oil escapes 
come within the word "damage" under s 8 of the Marine Pollution Act 1987 
(NSW)? 
 
Answer to question (i)(a): 
 
In that section, "damage" means a sudden change in the condition of the ship 
or its equipment that was the instantaneous consequence of some event, 
whether the event was external or internal to the ship or its equipment.  The 
Court considers the question otherwise not appropriate to answer.
