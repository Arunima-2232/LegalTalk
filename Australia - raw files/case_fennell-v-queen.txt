ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order made by the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Queensland on 21 July 2017 and in its place order that: 
 
 (a) the appeal be allowed;  
 
 (b) the appellant's conviction for murder be quashed; and  
 
 (c) a verdict of acquittal be entered. 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of Queensland
