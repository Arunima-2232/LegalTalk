CATCHWORDS 
 
Selected Seeds Pty Ltd v QBEMM Pty Limited  
 
Insurance – Product liability insurance – Insurance policy – Indemnity – 
Exclusion clause – Appellant seed merchant sold contaminated seed – Planting of 
seed by third party resulted in damage to property – Exclusion of liability caused 
by or arising from "the failure of any Product to correctly fulfil its intended use 
or function" – Proper construction of exclusion clause – Whether liability for 
damage arose out of failure of product to fulfil its intended use or function –  
Distinction between product failing to fulfil intended use or function and causing 
positive harm. 
 
Words and phrases – "intended use or function".
