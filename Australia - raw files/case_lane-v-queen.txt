Lane v The Queen 
[2018] HCA 28 
20 June 2018 
S308/2017 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside order 4 of the Court of Criminal Appeal of the Supreme 
Court of New South Wales dated 22 March 2017 and in lieu thereof 
order that: 
 
(a) the appellant's appeal to that Court be allowed; 
 
(b) the appellant's conviction and sentence be quashed; and 
 
(c) a new trial be had.
