Perre v Apand Pty Ltd [1999] HCA 36 
12 August 1999 
A27/1998 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Orders of the Full Court of the Federal Court made on 
21 November 1997 set aside; and in lieu thereof order that the appeal 
to that Court be allowed with costs. 
 
3. Order 5 of the orders made by Justice von Doussa on 20 and 
26 December 1996 varied to refer to the second and third respondents; 
orders 6 and 10 of those orders set aside. 
 
4. Remit the matter to a single judge of the Federal Court of Australia for 
further hearing. 
 
5. Costs of the first hearing to abide the outcome of the further hearing. 
 
6. Liberty to parties to apply to a single judge of the Federal Court of 
Australia to vary order 12 of the orders made by Justice von Doussa as 
may be appropriate. 
 
 
On appeal from the Federal Court of Australia
