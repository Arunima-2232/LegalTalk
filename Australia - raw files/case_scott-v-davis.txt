CATCHWORDS 
 
Scott v Davis 
 
Negligence – Vicarious liability – Agency – Whether owner of aircraft 
vicariously liable for negligence of person who flew aircraft with owner's consent 
for a social purpose connected with owner. 
 
Negligence – Vicarious liability – Aircraft – Motor vehicles – Negligent use of 
chattel of conveyance – Whether the principles in Launchbury v Morgans [1973] 
AC 127 apply – Non-delegable duties of care – Strict liability.
