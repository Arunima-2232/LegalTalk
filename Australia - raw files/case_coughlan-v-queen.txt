B60/2019 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The appeal be allowed.  
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Queensland made on 16 April 2019 and, in its place, order that:  
 
(a) the appeal be allowed;  
 
(b) the appellant's convictions for arson and attempted fraud be 
quashed; and  
 
(c) verdicts of acquittal be entered on each count.  
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of Queensland
