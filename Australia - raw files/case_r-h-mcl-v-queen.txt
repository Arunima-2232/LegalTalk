CATCHWORDS 
 
 
R H McL v The Queen 
 
 
Sentencing – Power of Court of Appeal to re- sentence on remaining convictions 
after quashing some convictions – Counts properly joined – No appeal against 
sentence by either Crown or appellant –  Principles of proportionality and totality 
– Whether Court of Appeal had power to increase the sentences of the appellant 
on the remaining convictions – Whether Court of Appeal gave adequate reasons 
for increase in sentence – Whether substituted sentence ordinarily subject to 
ceiling on total punishment so appellant not exposed to risk of increased 
punishment following a successful appeal. 
 
Words and phrases – "totality principle", "ceiling principle". 
 
Crimes Act 1958 (Vic), s 569(1). 
Sentencing Act 1991 (Vic), s 16(3A).
