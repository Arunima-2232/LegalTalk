Date of Hearing: 9 May 2024 
Date of Judgment: 9 October 2024 
S106/2023 
 
ORDER 
 
1. A writ of certiorari issue directed to the second defendants, the 
Federal Court of Australia and the Judges thereof, to quash the 
decision of the Full Court of the Federal Court of Australia made on 
14 July 2023 in proceeding NSD 726 of 2022 (HBSY Pty Ltd v Lewis 
[2023] FCAFC 109). 
 
2. A writ of mandamus issue directed to the second defendants 
commanding them to hear and determine the plaintiff's appeal from 
the whole of the judgment of the Supreme Court of New South Wales 
given on 24 June 2022 in proceeding 2019/263639.  
 
3. The first defendant pay the plaintiff's costs in the Full Court of the 
Federal Court of Australia and in this Court.
