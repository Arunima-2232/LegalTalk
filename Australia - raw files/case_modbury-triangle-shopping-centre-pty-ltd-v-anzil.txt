ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Orders of the Full Court of the Supreme Court of South Australia made on 
12 August 1999 set aside. In lieu thereof, order that the appeal to that Court 
be allowed with costs, the orders of Judge David made on 29 January 1999 
are set aside and the action is dismissed with costs.
