Waller v Hargraves Secured Investments Limited [2012] HCA 4 
29 February 2012 
S223/2011 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of New 
South Wales dated 11 November 2010 and in its place order that:  
 
(a) the appeal to that Court be allowed with costs; and  
 
(b) the orders of the Supreme Court of New South Wales dated 
12 November 2009 be set aside and in their place order that: 
 
(i) the proceedings be dismissed; and  
 
(ii) the respondent, Hargraves Secured Investments Limited, pay the 
costs of the appellant, Roslyn Edwina Waller, in that Court.  
 
3. The respondent pay the appellant's costs in this Court.
