CATCHWORDS 
 
Brownlee v The Queen 
 
Constitutional law – Trial by jury – State law permitting reduction during trial of 
number of jurors – State law permitting separation of jurors after jurors retire to 
consider verdict – Whether trial "by jury" within meaning of s 80 of Constitution. 
 
Constitutional law – Trial by jury – Waiver of requirements by accused –
Whether waiver possible – Whether waiver in fact – Whether leave required to 
reconsider past authority on constitutional question. 
 
Words and phrases – "trial ... by jury". 
 
Constitution, s 80. 
Judiciary Act 1903 (Cth), s 68. 
Jury Act 1977 (NSW), ss 22(a)(i), 54(b).
