Macleod v Australian Securities and Investments Commission 
[2002] HCA 37 
11 September 2002 
P55/2001 
 
ORDER 
 
1. The title of the respondent be amended to read "Australian Securities and 
Investments Commission". 
 
2. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
3. Set aside Orders 1 to 6 of the orders made by the Full Court of the Supreme 
Court of Western Australia on 13 April 2000 and in their place order that the 
appeal to that Court be dismissed with costs.
