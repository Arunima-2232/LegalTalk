HIGH COURT OF AUSTRALIA 
 
FRENCH CJ,

STATE OF VICTORIA APPELLANT 
 
AND 
 
TATTS GROUP LIMITED RESPONDENT 
 
 
Victoria v Tatts Group Limited 
[2016] HCA 5 
2 March 2016 
M83/2015 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Victoria made on 4 December 2014 and in their place order that: 
 
(a) the appeal be allowed with costs; 
 
(b) orders 1 and 2 of the orders of Hargrave J made on 27 June 
2014 be set aside, and in their place order that the proceeding 
be dismissed with costs; 
 
(c) the respondent pay the appellant $540,467,887.92 with 
interest, calculated from 27 June 2014. 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of Victoria
