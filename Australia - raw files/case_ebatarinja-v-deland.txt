Ebatarinja v Deland (D8-1998) [1998] HCA 62 
30 September 1998 
 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of the Northern Territory of 
Australia and, in lieu thereof, order that the appeal to that Court be 
allowed with costs and that prohibition issue directed to the first 
respondent prohibiting the further hearing of committal proceedings 
against the first appellant. 
 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of the Northern Territory
