CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Trustees of the Roman Catholic Church for the Diocese of Canberra and 
Goulburn (as St Anthony's Primary School) v Hadba 
 
 
Negligence − Standard of care −  Breach − Eight year old child injured when 
pulled from flying fox in school playground by fellow student −  Whether 
standard of care owed by school authority extends to the necessity to provide 
constant supervision over play equipment. 
 
Negligence − Reasonable practicability − Whether constant supervision of 
dangerous equipment reasonably practicable. 
 
Negligence − Causation − Whether a different system would have prevented the 
respondent's injuries.
