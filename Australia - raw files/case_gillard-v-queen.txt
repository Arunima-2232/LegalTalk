Gillard v The Queen 
[2014] HCA 16 
14 May 2014 
C20/2013 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
the Australian Capital Territory made on 18 April 2013 and, in its 
place, order that: 
(a)  the appeal be allowed in part; 
(b) the appellant's convictions on counts 13, 14, 16 and 18 of the 
indictment be quashed; and 
(c)  a new trial on counts 13, 14, 16 and 18 be had.
