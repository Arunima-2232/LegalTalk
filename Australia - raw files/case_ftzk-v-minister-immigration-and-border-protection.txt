FTZK v Minister for Immigration and Border Protection 
[2014] HCA 26 
27 June 2014 
M143/2013 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. The order of the Full Court of the Federal Court of Australia made 
on 6 May 2013 be set aside and, in its place, order that: 
 
(a) the proceedings be heard and determined as though instituted 
under s 476A of the Migration Act 1958 (Cth); 
 
(b) a writ of certiorari issue directed to the second respondent 
quashing the decision made on 23 May 2012; 
 
(c) a writ of mandamus issue directed to the second respondent 
requiring a differently constituted Administrative Appeals 
Tribunal to review according to law the decision of the first 
respondent to refuse the appellant a Protection (Class XA) 
visa; and 
 
(d) the first respondent pay the appellant's costs of the 
proceedings in the Federal Court of Australia.  
 
3. The first respondent pay the appellant's costs in this Court.
