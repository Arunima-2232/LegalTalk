Patel v The Queen

B11/2012 & B25/2011 
 
ORDER 
 
In matter B11/2012: 
 
Appeal dismissed. 
 
In matter B25/2011: 
 
1. Special leave to appeal granted. 
 
2. Appeal treated as instituted and heard instanter, and allowed. 
 
3. Set aside the order of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
Queensland made on 21 April 2011 and, in its place, order that: 
 
 (a) the appellant's appeal to that Court be allowed; 
 
 (b) the appellant's convictions for the manslaughter of Mervyn 
John Morris, James Edward Phillips and Gerardus 
Wilhelmus Gosewinus Kemps and for unlawfully doing 
grievous bodily harm to Ian Rodney Vowles be quashed; and 
 
 (c) a new trial be had.
