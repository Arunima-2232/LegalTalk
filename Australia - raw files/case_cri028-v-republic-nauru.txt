ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs.

2017, and in its place order that:   
 
 (a) the appeal be allowed; 
 
 (b) the decision of the Refugee Status Review Tribunal made on 
13 August 2015 be quashed; and 
 
 (c) the matter be remitted to the Refugee Status Review Tri bunal 
for redetermination according to law.
