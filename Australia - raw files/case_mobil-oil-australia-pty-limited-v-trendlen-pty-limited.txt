ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Supreme Court of New South Wales made on 27 
July 2005 and in their place order that: 
 
 (a) the plaintiff's notice of motion filed on 13 May 2005 be dismissed 
with costs;  
 
 (b) the proceedings not be continued as representative proceedings; 
and 
 
 (c) the plaintiff pay the defendant's costs of the defendant's notice  of 
motion filed on 7 October 2003.
