CATCHWORDS 
 
Fischer v Nemeske Pty Ltd 
 
Trusts – Trustees' powers – Power of advancement – Where trust property 
included shares in a company – Where value of shares recorded in "asset 
revaluation reserve" – Where trustee made resolution to distribute entire asset 
revaluation reserve to specified beneficiaries – Where trustee covenanted to pay 
specified beneficiaries on demand – Whether valid exercise of power to 
"advance" and "apply" trust capital or income – Whether trustee indebted to 
specified beneficiaries – Whether action for money had and received 
maintainable. 
 
Words and phrases – "advance", "apply", "pay", "pay or apply", "raise". 
 
Trustee Act 1925 (NSW), s 44. 
Trustee Act 1925 (UK), s 32.
