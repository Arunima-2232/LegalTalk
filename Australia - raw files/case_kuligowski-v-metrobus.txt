 
HIGH COURT OF AUSTRALIA

MAREK KULIGOWSKI APPELLANT

METROBUS RESPONDENT 
 
 
Kuligowski v Metrobus [2004] HCA 34 
3 August 2004 
P91/2003 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
2. Set aside the orders of the Full Court of the Supreme Court of Western 
Australia dated 24 June 2002 and in place thereof order that: 
 
(a) the appellant's appeal to the Full Court be allowed with costs; 
 
(b) the orders of the District Court of Western Australia dated 
18 October 2000 (in WC 93 D 775 of 1997 and CIV 4575 of 1998) 
be set aside and the matter remitted to the District Court for 
hearing; and 
 
(c) the respondent pay the appellant's costs of the said District Court 
proceedings both before Deputy Registrar Harman and 
Commissioner Ley.
