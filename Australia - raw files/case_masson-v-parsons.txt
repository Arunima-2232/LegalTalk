ORDER 
 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside orders 2 , 3, 4 and 8 made by the Full Court of the Family 
Court of Australia dated 28 June 2018 and, in their place, order that 
appeal number EA 111 of 2017 be dismissed. 
 
3. The first and second respondents pay the appellant's costs of the 
appeal to this Court.
