Bell Lawyers Pty Ltd v Pentelow 
[2019] HCA 29 
4 September 2019 
S352/2018 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside orders 1 to 4 of the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court 
of New South Wales made on 13 July 2018 and, in their place, order 
that: 
 
(a) the summons for judicial review be dismissed; and 
 
(b) the first respondent (Ms Pentelow) pay the costs of the 
appellant (Bell Lawyers Pty Ltd) in the District Court of New 
South Wales and the Court of Appeal of the Supreme Court of 
New South Wales. 
 
3. The first respondent pay the appellant's costs of the appeal to this 
Court.
