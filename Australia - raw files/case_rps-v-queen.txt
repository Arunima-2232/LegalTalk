3 February 2000 
S116/1998 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
 
2. Set aside the order of the Court of Criminal Appeal of New South 
Wales made on 13 August 1997. 
 
3. In place thereof, order that the appeal against convictions to the Court 
of Criminal Appeal of New South Wales be allowed, the convictions 
quashed and that there be a new trial on counts 4, 6, 7 and 8 of the 
indictment. 
 
On appeal from the Supreme Court of New South Wales
