ORDER 
 
1. Appeal allowed. 
2. Order of the Supreme Court of Queensland Court of Appeal of 29 October 
1999 set aside and in place thereof, order that: 
(a) the appellant's appeal to that Court be allowed;  
(b) the appellant's conviction on all counts be quashed; and 
(c) there be a retrial.
