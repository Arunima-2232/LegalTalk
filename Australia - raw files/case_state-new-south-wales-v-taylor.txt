CATCHWORDS 
 
State of New South Wales v Taylor 
 
Workers' Compensation – Election between permanent loss compensation and 
damages – Where worker seeks to revoke election to claim permanent loss 
compensation and to commence proceedings for recovery of damages – Where s 
151A(5) of the Workers Compensation Act 1987 (NSW) limits the availability of 
revocation of such election – Whether par (c) of s 151A(5) is satisfied if, at the 
time of the election, a reasonable person in the position of the injured person 
would have no cause to believe that further deterioration of the condition would 
probably occur. 
  
Statutes – Interpretation – s 151A(5)(c) of Workers Compensation Act 1987 
(NSW) – meaning of "no reasonable cause to believe". 
 
Words and phrases – "believe" – "would". 
 
Workers Compensation Act 1987 (NSW) s 151A.
