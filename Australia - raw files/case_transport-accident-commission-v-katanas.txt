CATCHWORDS 
 
Transport Accident Commission v Katanas 
 
Accident compensation – Transport accident – Statutory compensation scheme – 
Where respondent involved in motor vehicle accident and subsequently suffered 
mental disorder or disturbance – Where mental disorder or disturbance required 
to be "severe" to allow bringing of common law proceedings – Where respondent 
did not r equire inpatient psychiatric treatment – Where respondent found not to 
have suffered symptoms of psychological trauma at upper echelon of range – 
Whether severity of mental disorder or disturbance assessed only by reference to 
extent of treatment – Whether narrative test laid down in Humphries v Poljak 
[1992] 2 VR 129 followed by Court of Appeal.       
 
Words and phrases – "mental disturbance or disorder", "narrative test", "range or 
spectrum of comparable cases", "serious injury", "severe", "symptoms and 
consequences".
