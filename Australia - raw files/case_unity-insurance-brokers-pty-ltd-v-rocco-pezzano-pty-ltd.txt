CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Unity Insurance Brokers Pty Ltd v Rocco Pezzano Pty Ltd 
 
Insurance - Insurance Brokers - Breach of duty or contractual obligation by broker 
to disclose insured's full claims history to insurer - Compromise settlement by 
insured of claim against insurer and remainder of claimed damages sought from 
broker - Whether settlement reasonable - Whether sufficient evidence brought by 
insured to demonstrate the strength of the insurer's defence of non -disclosure - 
Whether broker's breach of duty or contractual obligation relevantly caused 
insured's loss - Whether settlement rendered the remainder of insured's damages 
too remote. 
 
Words and phrases - "A reasonable insurer". 
 
Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth), s 28.
