SINGLETON SHIRE COUNCIL      RESPONDENT 
 
Brodie v Singleton Shire Council [2001] HCA 29 
31 May 2001 
S44/1999 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Application for special leave to appeal granted. 
 
2. Appeal allowed with costs. 
 
3. Set aside the orders made by the New South Wales Court of Appeal on 
16 March 1999. 
 
4. Remit the matter to the New South Wales Court of Appeal for determination 
of the remaining issues on appeal. 
 
5. The costs of the appeal to the New South Wales Court of Appeal and of the 
trial to abide the outcome of that appeal.
