CATCHWORDS 
 
 
Manley v Alexander 
 
 
Negligence – Accident – Respondent struck and injured by a vehicle driven by 
the appellant – Respondent lying on road carriageway at time of accident – 
Respondent heavily intoxicated at time of accident – Appellant's attention drawn 
to a third person at the side of the road – Appellant continued to drive vehicle at 
same speed – Appellant changed vehicle direction by veering to the centre of the 
road – Whether appellant had exercised reasonable care in carrying out his duty 
to other road users.
