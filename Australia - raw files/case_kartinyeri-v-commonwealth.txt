Kartinyeri v The Commonwealth (A29/1997) [1998] HCA 22 
1 April 1998 
 
ORDER 
 
1. Order that the question reserved be answered as follows: on the facts 
pleaded in the Further Amended Statement of Claim and admitted in 
the Amended Defence, the question reserved for the consideration of 
the Full Court -  
 
 "Is the Hindmarsh Island Bridge Act  1997 or any part thereof 
invalid in that it is not supported by s 51(xxvi) of the Constitution 
or any other head of Commonwealth legislative power?" 
 
 Answer: No. 
 
2. Order that the plaintiffs pay the defendant's costs. 
 
3. Reserve the question of costs to be paid by the interveners and direct 
that any application made for an order against the interveners be made 
on notice filed and served within 14 days supported by written 
submissions, submissions in reply being filed within 10 days thereafter.
